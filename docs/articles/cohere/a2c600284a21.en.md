#### Building LLM tools for teachers

*Jussi Jauhiainen and Agustín Garagorry, University of Turku, Finland*

Millions of students worldwide struggle with one-size-fits-all education, while teachers lack evidence-based AI tools for personalization. Jussi, Agustín, and their team are transforming education by using LLMs to create personalized learning materials. Their research on LLM-powered student evaluation provides educators with evidence on effective tools, risks, and informed decision-making.

Starting in Finnish schools, they're expanding to English, Spanish, and African languages through partnerships.
