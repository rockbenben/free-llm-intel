### Join our community and shape AI with open science

- The strength of our open science community lies in its diversity and reach. With members spanning the globe we're creating a dynamic ecosystem where ideas flourish and breakthroughs are born.
- Find a specialized subgroup where your interests meet collective action. The Cohere Labs Open Science Community connects researchers focused on specific subjects like multilingual AI, safety, and more. When people with shared goals collaborate, individual curiosity transforms into breakthroughs that push the field forward.
- Research is real work, and in our Open Science Community you won't do it alone. Active participants find collaborators who can sharpen your methodology, and help you navigate the path to becoming a recognized AI researcher.
