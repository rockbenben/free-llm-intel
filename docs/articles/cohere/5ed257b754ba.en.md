# The world's leading open models, fully under your control

The right model for the right job, all under your jurisdiction.

![An abstract image with words overlayed reading "World's leading open models, under your control. Available in Model Vault"](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/rjtqmwfu/web3-prod/94683ad16fe28268fa577a6c84a3556bb7fc1a43-3840x2160.png?auto=format&fit=max&q=80&w=1130)

Key takeaways

- **Open models are now available on Model Vault**: Enterprises can run both Cohere's models and leading open models (such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Gemma 4, GPT-OSS, and Phi) on a fully managed, encrypted inference platform.
- **Zero data retention by default: **Neither prompts nor outputs are retained in Model Vault, providing a strong baseline of security.
- **Sovereign inference: **You retain control over four key elements: where your data lives (data residency and regulatory compliance), who can access it (isolated tenant with full privacy in Encrypted), which models you can use (the right model for each task rather than just one provider's offerings), and how your AI runs (not dictated by the provider).
- **Regional availability:** Model Vault is live in the United States, European Union, and Canada; Model Vault Encrypted is live in the US with EU and Canada support coming soon.

Sovereignty in AI means customers can choose, inspect, and deploy the models that best serve their needs without surrendering control to a single vendor, but that freedom should never come at the expense of security or privacy. Adding leading open models from Cohere and other providers across the globe inside of Model Vault gives our customers the flexibility they need to use the right tool for the job without sacrificing the standards that govern their business.

Every organization wants to use the best AI models available for the task at hand but many cannot. For a bank, a hospital or a government department, sending sensitive data to a shared API is often off the table. At the same time, managing inference for open or on-premise models may also not be possible due to capability or capacity constraints. Teams end up choosing between the model they want, the control they need, and the capacity they have to manage them.

They shouldn't have to. Today we're launching third party models inside Model Vault and Model Vault Encrypted, allowing Enterprises to run both Cohere’s models and leading open models on a fully managed and encrypted inference platform. Zero data retention of prompts and outputs is implemented by default in Model Vault.

### **Two levels of protection**

Model Vault provides the full inference stack managed through an isolated instance dedicated to your organization alone. Model Vault Encrypted goes further, keeping your data encrypted even while the model processes it.

**Model Vault:** Your models and workloads run on single tenant infrastructure. You choose the region, and your prompts and outputs stay there. Cohere manages the infrastructure so you get production-grade serving without building and running your own cluster.

**Model Vault Encrypted:** For your most sensitive work, Model Vault Encrypted protects data in use, not just at rest and in transit. Prompts and outputs are processed inside isolated environments and stay encrypted throughout using confidential computing. Not even Cohere can see what you send to a model or what it sends back. Neither can any cloud provider.

### **What sovereign inference means**

Sovereign inference means you decide how your AI runs, not your provider. It comes down to four things:

- **Where your data lives.** Run inference workloads in your preferred supported region to help meet your organization’s data residency and regulatory requirements.
- **Who can access it.** Model Vault keeps your workloads in a separate, isolated tenant. With Model Vault Encrypted, no one but you can see your prompts or outputs, including Cohere.
- **Which models you can use.** The model that best fits the task, not just the one that happens to be available through a single provider.

### **The right model for every job**

No single model is best at everything. Model Vault lets you pick the right one for each task, and run every one of them with the same protections.

**Cohere's models.** Command for generative and agentic work, Embed and Rerank for search and retrieval, Parse for documents, Transcribe for speech and Translate for multilingual workflows.

**High performing open models.** Alongside our own models, Model Vault runs the most-used open models, including DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (the most-used open model on OpenRouter), GLM 5.3 Flash, MiMo V2.6 Pro, Gemma 4, and NVIDIA Nemotron. GPT-OSS and Phi models are available too.

With Model Vault you can choose the best model for each task and run it within a unified, production inference stack.

### **FAQ**

**Which option is right for me?** Model Vault suits most regulated workloads that need an isolated tenant and in-region processing with a wide model catalogue. Choose Model Vault Encrypted for data sensitive workloads, when no one outside your organization, including Cohere, should be able to see your data.

**Can I run open models in Model Vault Encrypted?** Yes. Models like Gemma, GPT-OSS and Phi models run in Model Vault Encrypted today, alongside Cohere's Embed, Rerank, Transcribe and Translate. See the table above for the full list.

**How is Model Vault priced?** Pricing is by GPU type. Please visit [cohere.com/pricing](http://cohere.com/pricing) for details including on-demand (hourly) pricing and promotions. Reach out to sales for reserved capacity; monthly and annual commit pricing is available.
