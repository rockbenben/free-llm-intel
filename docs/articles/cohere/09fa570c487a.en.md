# Cohere and PwC partner to accelerate secure enterprise AI adoption

Bringing together secure, sovereign AI and transformation expertise to help organizations move from pilots to production

PwC and Cohere announced a global alliance, launching first in Canada, to help organizations deploy secure enterprise AI and turn AI investment into measurable business value.

The alliance brings together North, Cohere’s secure agentic AI platform, enterprise models, and search and retrieval capabilities with PwC’s industry, risk, regulatory, technology, and transformation expertise. Together, PwC and Cohere will help organizations identify high-value use cases, connect AI securely to trusted enterprise data and systems, and establish the governance and controls needed to deploy AI responsibly at scale.

Clients will be able to apply AI across enterprise search, knowledge access, deep research, decision support, and task automation, with deployment options including private cloud, on-premises, and air-gapped environments. The collaboration will help organizations maintain control over sensitive data and infrastructure while scaling AI across business workflows.

Learn more in PwC’s [press release](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/media/release/pwc-cohere-enterprise-ai-alliance.html).
