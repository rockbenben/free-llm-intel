# PitchBook in North: Private market intelligence for enterprise teams

Connect PitchBook to access company financials, funding history, investor activity, and market benchmarks - all within the workflows your teams already use in North.

![A purple banner showing a connected North and PitchBook.](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/rjtqmwfu/web3-prod/bd462488492399f45500e9b3b04295d8f1d23f06-3840x2160.jpg?auto=format&fit=max&q=80&w=1130)

We're excited to announce the launch of the [PitchBook Data Premium Connector](https://pitchbook.com/premium-connectors/cohere?utm_medium=display&utm_source=outreach&utm_campaign=partnership&utm_content=q4_2026_cohere) in North, bringing trusted private market intelligence directly into your existing AI workflows.

With the PitchBook Premium Connector in North, users can draw on PitchBook's institutional-grade data on companies, deals, funds, investors, market benchmarks, and more. Simply bring your PitchBook license, authenticate, and start equipping agents with PitchBook data.

## Using PitchBook and North

Ask questions, run analyses, and generate drafts that combine PitchBook data with your organization's internal knowledge.

**Corporate development and M&A: **Research transactions and draft memos that pair PitchBook financials with your internal strategy docs.

**Strategy and market research: **Map and automate competitive landscapes, track where capital is flowing in your space, and size markets with cited data you can refresh anytime.

**Finance and investor relations: **Benchmark valuations, funding rounds, and peer growth to draft board updates and investor materials grounded in current market data in North.

**Sales and partnerships: **Research prospects and partners before outreach, from recent funding and investors to leadership changes and growth signals, then use North to turn what you find into tailored messaging.

**Executive briefings: **Turn a leadership question into a sourced one-pager in minutes by combining PitchBook data with North's document mode.

## Built for enterprise control

PitchBook in North is bring-your-own-license, and the connector respects the entitlements and permissions your organization already has in place across PitchBook and North. Every figure North pulls from PitchBook comes with citations for quick human-in-the-loop verification.

## Availability

The PitchBook connector is available starting October 26, 2026 for North customers with an active PitchBook license and Single-Sign-On (SSO) enabled. Admins can enable it from their organization's connector settings.

To learn more, contact your Cohere account team, or talk to sales if you're new to North. Don’t have a PitchBook license? Visit [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/premium-connectors/cohere?utm_medium=display&utm_source=outreach&utm_campaign=partnership&utm_content=q4_2026_cohere) to learn more.
