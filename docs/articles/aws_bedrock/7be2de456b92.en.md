Clarified that prompt shortening depends on your evaluation. If the original prompt already scores at the maximum on your evaluation samples, or if the samples do not distinguish between candidate prompts, the original prompt is returned with a compression ratio of 1.0. Added guidance on choosing evaluation samples that give the optimizer room to improve. Updated the

Shorten prompts

page and the

Troubleshooting

page.
