# 使用 Amazon Bedrock 上的多模态基座模型与你的幻灯片对话 —— 第 3 部分

在本系列中，我们分享两种从文本、图像和图表等多模态数据中获取洞见的方法。在[第 1 部分](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/talk-to-your-slide-deck-using-multimodal-foundation-models-hosted-on-amazon-bedrock-and-amazon-sagemaker-part-1/)中，我们提出了一个“先嵌入、后推理”的解决方案，使用 [Amazon Titan Multimodal Embeddings](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/titan-multiemb-models.html) 基座模型（FM）把幻灯片中的单张幻灯片转换为 embedding。我们把 embedding 存入向量数据库，然后使用 [Large Language-and-Vision Assistant (LLaVA 1.5-7b)](https://llava-vl.github.io/) 模型，基于从向量数据库中检索到的最相似幻灯片，针对用户问题生成文本响应。第 1 部分使用了包括 [Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/)、[Amazon SageMaker](https://aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/) 和 [Amazon OpenSearch Serverless](https://aws.amazon.com/opensearch-service/features/serverless/) 在内的 AWS 服务。

在[第 2 部分](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/talk-to-your-slide-deck-using-multimodal-foundation-models-hosted-on-amazon-bedrock-and-amazon-sagemaker-part-2/)中，我们演示了另一种方法：“先推理、后嵌入”。我们使用 [Amazon Bedrock 上的 Anthropic Claude 3 Sonnet](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/claude/) 为幻灯片中的每一页生成文本描述。随后这些描述通过 [Amazon Titan Text Embeddings](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/titan-embedding-models.html) 模型转换为文本 embedding，并存入向量数据库。然后我们使用 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet，基于从向量数据库中检索到的最相关文本描述，针对用户问题生成答案。

在本文中，我们使用 [SlideVQA](https://github.com/nttmdlab-nlp/SlideVQA)[1] 提供的 ground truth 来评估两种方法的结果，该数据集是一个开源的视觉问答数据集。你可以测试两种方法并评估结果，为你的数据集找到最合适的方案。本系列的代码可在 [GitHub 仓库](https://github.com/aws-samples/multimodal-rag-on-slide-decks/tree/main)中找到。

## 方法对比

SlideVQA 是一批公开可用的幻灯片集合，每个集合由多张幻灯片（JPG 格式）和基于幻灯片内容的若干问题组成。它允许系统选定一组证据图片并回答问题。我们使用 SlideVQA 作为唯一权威来源来对比结果。在使用公开数据集时，务必遵守 [Amazon Bedrock 数据保护](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/data-protection.html)政策。

本文遵循下图所示流程。有关架构的更多细节，请参阅本系列第 1 部分和第 2 部分中的解决方案概述与设计。

[![处理流程](https://d2908q01vomqb2.cloudfront.net/f1f836cb4ea6efb2a0b1b99f41ad8b103eff4b59/2024/11/20/ML-16951-process-flow.png)](https://d2908q01vomqb2.cloudfront.net/f1f836cb4ea6efb2a0b1b99f41ad8b103eff4b59/2024/11/20/ML-16951-process-flow.png)

我们从 SlideVQA 中随机选取 100 个问题，构建一个样本数据集，用于测试第 1 部分和第 2 部分的解决方案。

样本数据集中问题的回答尽可能简洁，如下例所示：

```
"question": "What is the process by which the breaking of hydrogen bonds allows water to change from the liquid phase into the gaseous phase which has reached equilibrium with the liquid surface said to have done?"

"answer": "reached saturation"
```

Large language models（LLM）的响应则相当冗长：

```
According to the information provided in the images, the process by which the breaking of hydrogen bonds allows water to change from the liquid phase into the gaseous phase that has reached equilibrium with the liquid surface is said to have reached saturation.

The key points are:

1. Evaporation involves the breaking of hydrogen bonds that hold water molecules together in the liquid phase, allowing them to transition into the gaseous (vapor) phase.

2. Only the fastest moving water molecules with enough energy can overcome the hydrogen bonding and evaporate into the vapor phase.

3. The evaporation process that has reached equilibrium with the liquid surface, where the vapor pressure is balanced with the evaporation rate, is described as having reached saturation.

So in summary, the breaking of hydrogen bonds provides the mechanism for water molecules to gain enough energy to escape the liquid phase as vapor, and when this vapor has reached equilibrium with the liquid surface, it is said to have reached saturation.We updated the prompts in each approach to provide short responses instead of verbose responses. This helped match the output context length to the ground truth responses in the sample dataset.
```

下面几节简要讨论这些解决方案，并深入介绍每种方法的评估与定价。

## 方法 1：先嵌入，后推理

幻灯片被转换为 PDF 图片，每页一张，并使用 Amazon Titan Multimodal Embeddings 模型嵌入，得到 1,024 维的向量 embedding。embedding 存储在 OpenSearch Serverless 索引中，该索引作为我们 Retrieval Augmented Generation（RAG）解决方案的向量存储。embedding 通过 Amazon OpenSearch Ingestion pipeline 摄取。

每个问题都使用 Amazon Titan Multimodal Embeddings 模型转换为 embedding，并用这些 embedding 执行 OpenSearch 向量搜索。我们执行 k-nearest neighbor（k-NN）搜索，检索与问题最匹配的相关 embedding。OpenSearch 索引中记录的元数据包含指向最相关幻灯片对应图片的路径。

下面的 prompt 由问题与图片路径组合而成，发送给 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet，以简洁的答案回答问题：

```
Human: Your role is to provide a precise answer to the question in the <question></question> tags. Search the image provided to answer the question. Retrieve the most accurate answer in as few words as possible. Do not make up an answer. For questions that ask for numbers, follow the instructions below in the <instructions></instructions> tags. Skip the preamble and provide only the exact precise answer.

If the image does not contain the answer to the question below, then respond with two words only - "no answer".

Refer to the question and instructions below:

<question>
{question}
</question>


<instructions>
1. Search for relevant data and numbers in the charts and graphs present in the image.

2. If the image does not provide a direct answer to the user question, just say "no answer". Do not add statements like "The image does not provide..." and "It only mentions...", instead just respond with "no answer".

3. Do not add any tags in your answer.

4. Scan for the direct answer to the user question. If there is more than one direct answer, give everything that seems like a valid answer to the question in your response.

5. Search for the question deeply in the image. If the question asks about any data or statistics, look for it in charts, tables, graphs first, and then in texts. Check the headings in the image.

</instructions>

If the image does not contain the answer, or if image does not directly answer the user question, do not respond with "The image does not provide..." or anything similar. In this case, your response should always be "no answer" and nothing else.

Assistant: Here is my response to the question. I will give a direct and precise answer to the question if I find it and if not, I will say "no answer":
```

我们使用了 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet，而不是第 1 部分解决方案中提到的 LLaVA 1.5-7b。方法保持不变，仍是“先嵌入、后推理”，但为了简单以及保持两种方法之间的可比性，负责编译最终响应的模型发生了更换。

数据集中每个问题的回答都以 JSON 格式[记录](https://github.com/aws-samples/multimodal-rag-on-slide-decks/blob/main/Blog3-Comparison-of-Approaches/notebooks/responses-appr1.json)，并与 SlideVQA 提供的 ground truth 比较。

在 100 个问题的数据集上，该方法为 78% 的问题检索到了回答，最终回答的准确率为 50%。

## 方法 2：先推理，后嵌入

幻灯片被转换为 PDF 图片，每页一张，并传给 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet 生成文本描述。描述被发送给 Amazon Titan Text Embeddings 模型，生成 1,536 维的向量 embedding。embedding 通过 OpenSearch Ingestion pipeline 摄取到 OpenSearch Serverless 索引中。

每个问题都使用 Amazon Titan Text Embeddings 模型转换为 embedding，并用这些 embedding 执行 OpenSearch 向量搜索。我们执行 k-NN 搜索，检索与问题最匹配的相关 embedding。OpenSearch 索引中记录的元数据包含与最相关幻灯片对应的图片描述。

我们用问题与图片描述创建 prompt，并传给 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet 以获得精确答案。以下是 prompt 模板：

```
Human: Your role is to provide a precise answer to the question in the <question></question> tags. Search the summary provided in the <summary></summary> tags to answer the question. Retrieve the most accurate answer in as few words as possible. Do not make up an answer. For questions that ask for numbers, follow the instructions below in the <instructions></instructions> tags. Skip the preamble and provide only the exact precise answer.

If the summary does not contain the answer to the question below, then respond with two words only - "no answer".

Refer to the question, summary, and instructions below:

<question>
{question}
</question>

<summary>
{summary}
</summary>

<instructions>
1. Search for relevant data and numbers in the summary.

2. If the summary does not provide a direct answer to the user question, just say "no answer". Do not add statements like "The summary does not specify..." and "I do not have enough information...", instead just respond with "no answer".

3. Do not add any tags in your answer.

4. Scan for the direct answer to the user question. If there is more than one direct answer, give everything that seems like a valid answer to the question in your response.

</instructions>

If the summary does not contain the answer, or if summary does not directly answer the user question, do not respond with "The summary does not provide..." or anything similar. In this case, your response should always be "no answer" and nothing else.

Assistant: Here is my response to the question. I will give a direct and precise answer to the question if I find it and if not, I will say "no answer":
```

数据集中每个问题的回答都以 JSON 格式[记录](https://github.com/aws-samples/multimodal-rag-on-slide-decks/blob/main/Blog3-Comparison-of-Approaches/notebooks/responses-appr2.json)，以便于比较。回答会与 SlideVQA 提供的 ground truth 比较。

采用这种方法，在样本数据集的 100 个问题中，有 75% 的问题检索到了回答，最终回答的准确率为 44%。

## 结果分析

在我们的测试中，两种方法与样本数据集中问题的匹配结果都不超过 50%。样本数据集包含随机选取的幻灯片，覆盖零售、医疗、学术、技术、个人和旅行等广泛主题。因此，对于像“可以使用哪些工具？举几个例子”这类缺乏额外上下文的一般性问题，最近匹配可能从各种主题中检索到回答，尤其当所有 embedding 都摄取到同一个 OpenSearch 索引时，这会导致结果不准确。使用混合搜索、基于元数据的预过滤以及重新排序等技术，可以提高检索准确率。

解决方案之一是检索更多结果（增大 k 值）并重新排序，保留其中最相关的那些；这种技术称为 *reranking*。我们在本文后面会分享更多提高结果准确率的方法。

在我们的分析中，发送给 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet 的[最终 prompt](https://github.com/aws-samples/multimodal-rag-on-slide-decks/tree/main/Blog3-Comparison-of-Approaches/prompts) 包含了以下指令：以尽可能少的字数给出简洁答案，以便与 ground truth 比较。你的响应将取决于你发送给 LLM 的 prompt。

## 结论

在本系列的第 1 部分和第 2 部分中，我们探索了如何使用 Amazon Titan Multimodal Embeddings、Amazon Titan Text Embeddings 和 Anthropic 的 Claude 3 Sonnet 这类多模态 FM 的能力。在本文中，我们从准确率与定价两个角度对比了这些方法。

本系列各部分的代码都可在 GitHub 仓库中找到。我们鼓励你部署两种方法，并探索 Amazon Bedrock 上可用的不同 Anthropic Claude 模型。无论采用哪种方法，你都可以用自己组织的幻灯片内容发现新信息、挖掘新视角。对比两种方法，为你的幻灯片找出更好的 workflow。

随着生成式 AI 的快速发展，有多种方法可以改进结果并推进这一问题。我们正在探索执行混合搜索，并通过从问题中提取实体来增加搜索过滤器，以改进结果。本系列第 4 部分将详细探讨这些概念。
