# Accelerate intelligent document processing with generative AI on AWS

***Update March 2026 (v0.5.0):** This release unifies deployment into a single solution with two runtime-switchable modes — Amazon Bedrock Data Automation (BDA) for standard use cases and Bedrock Pipeline mode for complex documents requiring custom logic. New capabilities include Test Studio for accuracy/cost benchmarking, an AI Agent Companion for analytics, built-in human-in-the-loop (HITL) review, business rule validation, intelligent document discovery, and custom model integration via Lambda hooks. For the full list of changes, see [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).*

Every day, organizations process millions of documents, including invoices, contracts, insurance claims, medical records, and financial statements. Despite the critical role these documents play, an [estimated 80–90%](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/tapping-power-unstructured-data) of the data they contain is unstructured and largely untapped, hiding valuable insights that could transform business outcomes. Despite advances in technology, many organizations still rely on manual data entry, spending countless hours extracting information from PDFs, scanned images, and forms. This manual approach is time-consuming, error-prone, and prevents organizations from scaling their operations and responding quickly to business demands.

Although generative AI has made it easier to build proof-of-concept document processing solutions, the journey from proof of concept to production remains fraught with challenges. Organizations often find themselves rebuilding from scratch when they discover their prototype can’t handle production volumes, lacks proper error handling, doesn’t scale cost-effectively, or fails to meet enterprise security and compliance requirements. What works in a demo with a handful of documents often breaks down when processing thousands of documents daily in a production environment.

In this post, we introduce our open source [GenAI IDP Accelerator](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/README.md)—a tested solution that we use to help customers across industries address their document processing challenges. Automated document processing workflows accurately extract structured information from documents, reducing manual effort. We will show you how this ready-to-deploy solution can help you build those workflows with generative AI on AWS in days instead of months.

## Understanding intelligent document processing

Intelligent document processing (IDP) encompasses the technologies and techniques used to extract and process data from various document types. Common IDP tasks include:

- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)** – Converting scanned documents and images into machine-readable text
- **Document classification** – Automatically identifying document types (such as invoices, contracts, or forms)
- **Data extraction** – Pulling structured information from unstructured documents
- **Assessment** – Evaluating the quality and confidence of extracted data
- **Summarization** – Creating concise summaries of document content
- **Evaluation** – Measuring accuracy and performance against expected outcomes

These capabilities are critical across industries. In financial services, organizations use IDP to process loan applications, extract data from bank statements, and validate insurance claims. Healthcare providers rely on IDP to extract patient information from medical records, process insurance forms, and handle lab results efficiently. Manufacturing and logistics companies use IDP to process invoices and purchase orders, extract shipping information, and handle quality certificates. Government agencies use IDP to process citizen applications, extract data from tax forms, manage permits and licenses, and enforce regulatory compliance.

## The generative AI revolution in IDP

Traditional IDP solutions relied on template-based extraction, regular expressions, and classical machine learning (ML) models. Though functional, these approaches required extensive setup, struggled with document variations, and achieved limited accuracy on complex documents.

The emergence of large language models (LLMs) and generative AI has fundamentally transformed IDP capabilities. Modern AI models can understand document context, handle variations without templates, achieve near-human accuracy on complex extractions, and adapt to new document types with minimal examples. This shift from rule-based to intelligence-based processing means organizations can now process different document types with high accuracy, dramatically reducing the time and cost of implementation.

## GenAI IDP Accelerator

We’re excited to share the [GenAI IDP Accelerator](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/README.md)—an open source solution that transforms how organizations handle document processing by dramatically reducing manual effort and improving accuracy. Combined with other AWS AI services, it provides a flexible, scalable starting point for enterprises to build document automation tailored to their specific needs.

## Solution overview

The GenAI IDP Accelerator is a modular, serverless solution that automatically converts unstructured documents into structured, actionable data. Built entirely on AWS services, it provides enterprise-grade scalability, security, and cost-effectiveness while requiring minimal setup and maintenance. Its configuration-driven design helps teams quickly adapt prompts, extraction templates, and validation rules for their specific document types without touching the underlying infrastructure.

The solution follows a modular pipeline that enriches documents at each stage, from OCR to classification, to extraction, to assessment, to summarization, and ending with evaluation.

You can deploy and customize each step independently, so you can optimize for your specific use cases while maintaining the benefits of the integrated workflow.

The following diagram illustrates the solution architecture. A single unified deployment supports two processing modes — switchable at runtime through configuration.

![](https://d2908q01vomqb2.cloudfront.net/f1f836cb4ea6efb2a0b1b99f41ad8b103eff4b59/2026/03/02/IDP.UnifiedPatterns.drawio-1024x880.png)

Refer to the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/architecture.md) for additional details.

Some of the key features of the solution include:

- **Serverless architecture** – Built on [AWS Lambda](https://aws.amazon.com/lambda/), [AWS Step Functions](https://aws.amazon.com/step-functions/), and other serverless technologies for queueing, concurrency management, and retries to provide automatic scaling and pay-per-use pricing for production workloads of many sizes
- **Generative AI-powered document packet splitting and classification** – Intelligent document [classification](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/classification.md) using Amazon Bedrock Data Automation or Amazon Bedrock multimodal FMs, including support for multi-document packets and packet splitting
- **Advanced AI key information extraction** – Key information [extraction](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/extraction.md) using Amazon Bedrock Data Automation or Amazon Bedrock multimodal FMs with optional [agentic extraction](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/lib/idp_common_pkg/idp_common/extraction/agentic_idp.py) for self-correcting, schema-compliant output
- **Unified processing architecture** (introduced in v0.5.0) – A single deployment supports two processing modes, switchable at runtime via the `use_bda` configuration toggle — no redeployment needed. Use [Test Studio](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/test-studio.md) to compare accuracy and cost across both modes and find the optimal approach for your documents:  BDA mode – Uses [Amazon Bedrock Data Automation](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/bda/), a fully managed service that offers rich out-of-the-box features, ease of use, and straightforward per-page pricing. Recommended for most use cases Bedrock Pipeline mode – Uses [Amazon Textract](https://aws.amazon.com/textract/) and [Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/) with Amazon Nova, Anthropic’s Claude, or custom fine-tuned models for full control over each processing step. Ideal for complex documents requiring custom logic, agentic extraction, or custom model integration
- **Few-shot learning** – Improve accuracy for classification and extraction by providing [few-shot examples](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/few-shot-examples.md) to guide the AI models
- **Confidence assessment** – AI-powered quality assurance that [evaluates extraction field confidence](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/assessment.md), used to indicate documents for human review, with bounding box visualization for visual verification of extracted data
- **Human-in-the-loop (HITL) review** – Built-in [human review](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/human-review.md) system integrated directly into the web UI with role-based access control (Admin and Reviewer personas), review ownership to prevent concurrent edits, and section-level review workflows
- **Web user interface** – [Responsive web UI](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/web-ui.md) for monitoring document processing, viewing results, and managing configurations
- **Knowledge base integration** – [Query processed documents](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base.md) using natural language through [Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/knowledge-bases/)
- **Built-in evaluation** – Framework to [evaluate](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/evaluation.md) and improve accuracy against baseline data
- **Test Studio** – Unified web interface for [managing test sets, running tests, and analyzing results](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/test-studio.md) with side-by-side comparison, interactive charts, and auto-deployed benchmark datasets for immediate testing
- **AI Agent Companion** – Interactive [AI assistant](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/agent-companion-chat.md) with specialized agents for document analytics (natural language to SQL with visualizations), error analysis and troubleshooting, and code intelligence, with real-time streaming responses
- **Rule validation** – Automated [validation of extracted data](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/rule-validation.md) against configurable business rules and compliance criteria, supporting use cases such as healthcare prior authorization, loan compliance, and contract verification
- **Intelligent document discovery** – Automatically [analyzes document samples](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/discovery.md) to identify structure, field types, and organizational patterns, then generates configuration to accelerate onboarding of new document types
- **Analytics and reporting database** – Centralized [analytics database](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/reporting-database.md) for tracking processing metrics, accuracy trends, and cost optimization across document workflows, and for analyzing extracted document content using [Amazon Athena](https://aws.amazon.com/athena)
- **Developer-friendly python package** – For data science and engineering teams who want to experiment, optimize, or integrate the IDP capabilities directly into their workflows, the solution’s core logic is available through the [idp_common Python package](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/lib/idp_common_pkg/README.md)
- **IDP CLI and Python SDK** – [Command-line interface](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/idp-cli.md) and [Python SDK](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/idp-sdk.md) for batch document processing, stack management, and load testing for programmatic integration into CI/CD pipelines, Lambda functions, and custom workflows
- **Custom model integration** – [Lambda Hook Inference](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/lambda-hook-inference.md) enables integration with any LLM — models hosted on [Amazon SageMaker](https://aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/), Amazon ECS, Amazon EC2, or external APIs — in any pipeline step
- **MCP integration** – [Model Context Protocol](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/mcp-integration.md)) support enables external applications to access IDP capabilities through secure OAuth 2.0 authenticated endpoints
- **No-code configuration** – Customize document types, extraction fields, and processing logic through [configuration](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/configuration.md), editable in the web UI. Manage multiple named [configuration versions](https://github.com/aws-solutions-library-samples/accelerated-intelligent-document-processing-on-aws/blob/main/docs/configuration-versions.md) as complete snapshots with compare, activate, and rollback capabilities, enabling safe experimentation and controlled rollouts
