Clarified RPM quota behavior across Amazon Bedrock endpoints. The

bedrock-mantle

endpoint does not expose per-model RPM quotas; throttling is governed by token-based quotas. On the

bedrock-runtime

endpoint, RPM quotas are model-specific — some models, including Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 and Claude Opus 4.8, are not subject to an RPM quota. For models that do have an RPM quota, see the Service Quotas console.
