# Your complete guide to AWS Quick at AWS re:Invent 2026

AWS Quick is the AI workspace built only for work, where the dashboards, agents, and automations one person creates are available to everyone on the team. Built on AWS with enterprise security and governance from the start, Quick connects to the data and applications you use every day. At AWS re:Invent 2026 (November 30 to December 4), customers including Allianz, Cox Automotive, Mondelez, DXC, and Southwest Airlines, join AWS leaders on stage to share how they govern, extend and scale Quick across their teams. Whether you’re a business or technology leader, a builder, or part of an IT, security, data, or analytics team, use this guide to find the sessions that fit your goals.

[Explore the session catalog and learn more](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=quick).

Learn why existing solutions fell short, how Quick’s native governance model met their requirements without custom engineering, and what the transition looked like from decision to deployment.

## Chalk talks

Whiteboard architectures and trade-offs with the AWS Quick product and solutions teams. Bring your toughest questions.

**[BIZ306: Bring your own ontology: Ground AWS Quick in enterprise context](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ306)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 10:00 AM PST
*Speakers: Tom Ron, Fatima Khakwani*

Enterprise questions rarely have answers in a single document. Your business runs on connections: customers to products, teams to processes, and risks to regulations. But vector search doesn’t see any of that. In this chalk talk, we show how to ground AWS Quick in your enterprise context by integrating it with a knowledge graph built from your domain knowledge. We’ll walk through how we built this pattern using GraphRAG for real customers and the results they achieved. Then we’ll open the whiteboard. Bring your toughest use case and we’ll sketch your ontology live, showing how Quick would traverse it to deliver better answers.

**[BIZ323: Decision frameworks, evaluation, and cost management with AWS Quick](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ323)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 10:30 AM PST
 Tuesday, Dec 1 | 2:30 PM PST *(repeat)*
*Speakers: Sagar Rao, Sandhya Khanderia*

Moving from one agent to enterprise-wide automation means choosing the right path (Flows, Automate, Custom Agents, persistent agents) and the right trigger model. In this chalk talk, we whiteboard a decision framework that bakes in evaluation and cost management from the start, not as afterthoughts. We’ll connect orchestration across 25+ connectors, skills, and workstreams to measurable outcomes and dollars. Bring your automation backlog. You’ll leave with a decision matrix, eval harness, and a return on investment (ROI) story.

**[BIZ307: Architect AI-powered analytics with AWS Quick across your data platforms](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ307)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 12:00 PM PST
*Speakers: Vignessh Baskaran, Jackson Dowden*

As organizations adopt AI-powered analytics, they need architectures that connect business intelligence (BI) and conversational AI experiences to their existing data platforms. In this session we’ll diagram architectural patterns for connecting AWS Quick to structured data sources and integrating with third-party catalogs and semantic layers. We’ll compare the available connectivity approaches, including data sources and action connectors based on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and discuss when to use each. Considerations will span production use cases like BI dashboards, embedded analytics, text-to-SQL Q&A, and agentic workflows. Together we’ll tackle real-world integration challenges including authentication, performance optimization, and governance. This session is ideal for architects and technical leaders looking to build AI-powered analytics across their data platforms.

**[BIZ404: Teaching Quick to remember and connect with memory and knowledge graphs](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ404)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 1:30 PM PST
*Speakers: Thomas Delteil, Adi Kalyanpur*

Every conversation with an AI assistant starts from scratch. Your agent doesn’t remember what worked yesterday, can’t tell you who owns a project without searching again, and repeats the same mistakes because failures are never captured. In this chalk talk, we show how AWS Quick breaks out of statelessness with two primitives: a continuous-learning memory layer where a background agent extracts reusable knowledge after each session and scores it with a probabilistic confidence model, and a personal knowledge graph that connects people, projects, decisions, and evidence across your tools so the agent has a map of your world before it reads a single document. We’ll whiteboard the architecture and the tradeoffs that shaped it, then open it up. Bring your hardest questions about memory staleness, confidence calibration, graph noise, or making agents that actually get better over time.

**[BIZ305: Security: Zero-trust identity and data protection in the agentic era](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ305)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 2:30 PM PST
*Speakers: Priya Kakarla, Munish Gopal*

Security reviews stall AI rollouts more than any capability gap, and agentic AI raises the bar. This chalk talk goes past “is it secure?” to the architecture that gets a CISO to yes. We’ll whiteboard the full identity path: single sign-on (SSO) through providers like Okta and Ping, with end-user identity propagated to the data so authorization is enforced per user, in real time, across 1P AWS and 3P sources. Then we extend that trust to agents: agent identity, deterministic policy checks on every tool call, and data protection: Purview DLP, default guardrails, deny-by-default, and full SIEM auditability.

**[BIZ310: Secure and scalable multi-tenant deployment patterns for AWS Quick](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ310)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 3:00 PM PST
*Speakers: Chin Rane, Vineet Kachhawaha*

As organizations scale their adoption of AWS Quick across business units, subsidiaries, and partner ecosystems, deploying a secure multi-tenant architecture becomes critical. This chalk talk explores proven deployment patterns that balance tenant isolation, governance, and operational efficiency when rolling out AWS Quick at enterprise scale. We will walk through two primary isolation strategies: multi-account separation using AWS Organizations for environment-level boundaries (dev/staging/prod), and namespace-based logical isolation within a single account for user-cohort segmentation.

**[BIZ304: Building and governing apps on Quick](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ304)**
 Monday, Nov 30 | 4:00 PM PST
*Speakers: Shane Thompson, Art Chan*

Anyone can generate apps from a sentence. The hard part is governing them and getting them in front of the right users. This chalk talk whiteboards the full lifecycle of AWS Quick Apps: building them from natural language with no separate hosting, wiring in data and actions through MCP, and the three sharing paths: private, public URLs, and enterprise sharing gated behind admin approval workflows.

**[BIZ303: One context layer, every surface: Enterprise knowledge for agents that work anywhere](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ303)**
 Thursday, Dec 3 | 12:00 PM PST
*Speakers: Suren Raju, Josh DeMuth*

Your AI agents are only as smart as the data they can reach. In most enterprises, that data is scattered across dozens of systems, siloed, ungoverned, and invisible to the agents that need it most. In this chalk talk, we architect a context layer from the ground up: connecting enterprise knowledge into a unified index, then enriching that foundation with a knowledge graph that maps organizational relationships, business rules, and domain context, giving agents permission-scoped, relationship-aware intelligence at runtime. Then we take that intelligence to where users work: browser, desktop, mobile, collaboration tools, and productivity suites. Each surface independently governed yet sharing one agent identity, one memory, and one set of guardrails. Bring your architecture. We’ll whiteboard the end-to-end path together.

**[INV001: Ctrl+Alt+Ship: Hands-On with AWS Quick, Kiro, and Amazon Connect Customer](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=ctrl%20alt%20ship)**
 Walk-up, available all week
*Speakers: Michelle Koh, Anupa Bhattacharyya, Christopher Lott *

Ctrl+Alt+Ship is a walk-up, self-paced lab area open all week (Nov 30 – Dec 4) in the Venetian Level 2 ballroom. No reservation is needed and workstations are provided. Attendees pick a track (Kiro, Quick or Connect) and a level, 200 or 400. Both Quick labs are business simulations where Quick connects to the company’s business systems through MCP. In the L200 Kōji Fresh lab, attendees lead the response to a product-safety crisis at a fictional Singapore food company. They investigate across email, CRM, social and supply chain, brief the board and supplier, run a recall across three systems from one instruction, and turn five data exports into board-ready charts. In the L400 SkyNova Airlines lab, 14 passengers have fallen ill overnight and an influencer is blaming the airline. Attendees use Quick across six systems to find the pattern and brief the board. They then rebook stranded passengers within a hard budget, build a Flow that runs the recovery for a grounded flight, and ship a Quick App for the airport transfer desk. Facilitators will be on hand to help.

**[BIZ312: Build AI-powered analytics with conversational BI on AWS Quick](https://registration.awsevents.com/flow/awsevents/reinvent2026/eventcatalog/page/eventcatalog?search=BIZ312)**

You leave with a working banking command center that agents built, and that you can rebuild in your own account using conversational analytics.
