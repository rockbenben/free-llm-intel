## March 10, 2026  

*     Released `gemini-embedding-2-preview`, our first multimodal embedding model.
  It supports text, image, video, audio, and PDF inputs, mapping all
  modalities into a unified embedding space. To learn more, see
  [Embeddings](/docs/embeddings).

*     Deprecation announcement: The `gemini-2.5-flash-lite-preview-09-2025` model
  will be [shut down](/docs/deprecations) on March 31, 2026.
