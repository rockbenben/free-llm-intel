## March 25, 2026  

*   Launched [Lyria 3](/docs/music-generation) music generation models:
  [`lyria-3-clip-preview`](/docs/models/lyria-3-clip-preview) (30-second
  clips) and [`lyria-3-pro-preview`](/docs/models/lyria-3-pro-preview)
  (full-length songs). Both models accept text and image inputs and generate
  high-quality, 48kHz stereo audio. See the [Music
  generation](/docs/music-generation) guide for details and code samples.
