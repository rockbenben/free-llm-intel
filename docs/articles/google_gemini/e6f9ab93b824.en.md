## June 24, 2026  

*   **Computer Use**: Launched public preview support for the [Computer
  Use](/docs/computer-use) tool in Gemini 3.5 Flash. This release includes
  simplified actions with intents, built-in support for browser, mobile, and
  desktop environments, configurable safety policies, and advanced prompt
  injection detection.
