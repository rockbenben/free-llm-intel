## September 3, 2026  

*     **Lyria 3.5 generally available (GA)**: Released the next generation of
  Google's music generation model:

  *   [`lyria-3.5`](/docs/models/lyria-3.5):
    Full-length song generation with improved musical coherence, natural
    vocals, and fine-grained duration and structural control.

  The model supports text and image inputs and generates high-fidelity 44.1
  kHz stereo audio. See the [Music generation](/docs/music-generation)
  guide for details and code samples.
