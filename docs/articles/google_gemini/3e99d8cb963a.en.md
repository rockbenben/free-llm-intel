## September 1, 2026  

*     **Agentic video understanding**: Released agentic video understanding for
  Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite across the Interactions and
  GenerateContent APIs. The model dynamically navigates video timelines,
  requesting transcripts, frames, or audio tracks on demand. This approach
  uses up to 88% fewer tokens for long-form content compared to static
  processing.

  To get started, see the [Agentic video
  understanding](/docs/video-understanding#agentic-video-understanding) guide.
