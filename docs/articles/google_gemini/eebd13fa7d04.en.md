## December 11, 2025  

*     Launched the Interactions API. This API provides a unified interface for
  interacting with Gemini models and agents. To learn more, see the
  [Interactions API](/docs/interactions-overview) guide.

*     Launched the Gemini Deep Research agent in preview. It can autonomously
  plan, execute, and synthesize results for multi-step research tasks. See the
  [Deep Research](/docs/deep-research) guide for details.
