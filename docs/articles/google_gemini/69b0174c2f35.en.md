## June 17, 2026  

*   **Streaming support for speech generation**: Streaming via
  `streamGenerateContent` (and `stream: true` in the Interactions API) is now
  supported for the `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview` model. To learn more, see
  the [Text-to-Speech](/docs/speech-generation#streaming) guide.
