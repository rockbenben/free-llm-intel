# ModCon 2026: Compute Unlocked

*Modular's *[*annual developer conference*](https://www.modular.com/modcon)* returns to San Francisco on August 18th. One day of launches, live demos, and the people building and making big bets on the AI stack.*

On August 18th, the Modular community gathers in San Francisco for [ModCon 2026: Compute Unlocked](https://www.modular.com/modcon?utm_source=ModConblog). ModCon is an intimate one-day conference built around a single idea: solving the hardware scarcity problem.

[Registration opens today](https://www.modular.com/modcon?utm_source=ModConblog), Wednesday June 17th. Early-bird pricing is $299 through July 1st, then $450. We’re opening up just 300 spots for this exclusive event.

## Why "Compute Unlocked"

For years, the AI stack has been locked to specific hardware and switching meant rewriting everything you'd already shipped. At ModCon 2026, we’ll enable hardware flexibility and showcase what changes when the same model, code, and container run across NVIDIA, AMD, and new hardwares to be announced, with the performance and cost numbers to back it up.

Be the first to learn about exclusive new launches across Modular’s software stack including Mojo, MAX, and Modular Cloud. Join us and an incredible lineup of speakers at [ModCon 2026.](https://www.modular.com/modcon#speakers?utm_source=ModConblog)

## Who's speaking

Phase 1 of our speaker lineup includes builders and leaders from up and down the AI stack:

- Chris Lattner, Co-Founder and CEO, Modular

[See the full announced speaker list ](https://www.modular.com/modcon#speakers?utm_source=ModConblog)- with more coming soon.

## The day at a glance

ModCon runs 9:00am to 7:00pm:

- **Morning:** keynotes and luminary fireside chat
- **Midday:** lunch with live demos
- **Afternoon:** panel discussions, technical deep dives, and hands-on breakout sessions
- **Evening:** a reception to close out the day

## Get your ticket

Every ticket includes keynotes, expo and demo access, snacks, catered lunch, the evening reception, and Modular merch.

Early bird is $299 until July 1, then it's $450. Join us August 18th for this exclusive event.
