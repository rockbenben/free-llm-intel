# Modverse #52: Advancing AI Together — Community Projects & Platform Milestones

Inaara Walji

Community

The Modular universe is buzzing! From next-level community projects to recognition across the AI and developer space, here’s the latest from our growing ecosystem.

## Community Innovations

The Modular community never fails to impress us with its creativity and innovation. Developers from around the world share projects that push the boundaries of what’s possible with MAX and Mojo, from tools that make AI development easier to experiments that explore new frontiers in performance. Discover what the Modular community has been building:

- In our recent community meeting, we heard from Martin Vuyk on his generic FFT implementation in Mojo, which delivered up to 18× faster performance than cuFFT in his benchmarks, and from Gabriel de Marmiesse on his MAX backend for PyTorch. Catch the full details in our [October Community Meeting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OHgKUL65yU) recording.
- mewmix’s project is a pure [Mojo implementation of Keccak-256](https://forum.modular.com/t/keccak-256-in-mojo/2419), complete with a self-contained test harness and reproducible test vectors.
- Photon created [SciJo](https://forum.modular.com/t/scijo-scientific-computing-library-for-mojo/2386?utm_source=chatgpt.com), a high-performance scientific computing library for Mojo, providing SciPy-like functionality with the speed and efficiency of native Mojo code.
- Laranzu created [a Julia fractal image generator in Mojo](https://forum.modular.com/t/example-fractal-julia-set-in-mojo-and-mojo-gpu/2374) for both CPU and GPU, providing an accessible example for Python programmers and demonstrating Mojo’s impressive performance compared to C++ and Python.
- Sören developed [duckdb.mojo](https://forum.modular.com/t/duckdb-mojo-mojo-bindings-for-duckdb/2314), providing Mojo bindings that let developers tap into DuckDB’s high‑performance analytical database.
- Constantin G created [Butterfly](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/logicalguess_mojo-quantumcomputing-quantumsimulator-activity-7390921824420888576-juTr/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAADYG-sBeXCycWTGv55OvhuNplb0W7AgorY), a new Mojo-based qubit simulator that already delivers 10–15x faster performance than Qiskit while showcasing how elegantly Mojo handles core quantum operations.
- Ash Vardanian’s [Scaling Elections](https://ashvardanian.com/posts/scaling-elections/) explores how the computationally expensive Schulze voting method can be massively accelerated using GPUs and Mojo — enabling fairer ranked‑choice elections to scale efficiently.
- spluta created [MMMAudio](https://forum.modular.com/t/mmmaudio-creative-coding-audio-environment/2359), a creative-coding audio environment that uses Python for control and Mojo for audio, enabling both high-level instrument building and low-level DSP in one unified workflow.

💡 We love seeing what you create! Share your MAX or Mojo projects in [the forum under the Community Showcase category](https://forum.modular.com/c/community-showcase/8) so we can highlight them in the next edition.

## Modular Making Waves

Modular continues to make waves in AI and high-performance computing, from leadership appearances to benchmarks demonstrating MAX and Mojo’s capabilities.

- Independent benchmarking by Meta AI recently showcased at Triton Conf shows Mojo matching or outperforming CUDA and significantly outpacing Triton and TileLang DSLs, highlighting Modular’s high-performance capabilities.

- Inworld TTS 1 MAX, powered by Modular Platform, delivers top-tier performance on the [Artificial Analysis Speech Arena Leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/text-to-speech/leaderboard), surpassing benchmarks set by MiniMax’s Speech-02 series and OpenAI’s TTS-1 series. Modular is proud to power the infrastructure behind this achievement.

- Chris Lattner, Co-Founder & CEO of Modular, shares his Swift-to-Mojo journey 🔥, his vision for bridging AI compute across CPUs and GPUs, and how AI can elevate, not replace, software craftsmanship. Featured on[ The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/from-swift-to-mojo-and-high-performance) and in a conversation with[ fast.ai co-founder Jeremy Howard](https://lnkd.in/dQSuYkd4)
- Tim Davis, President & Co-Founder of Modular, presented in a webinar on building scalable AI systems, highlighting how Modular, AMD, Supermicro, and TensorWave accelerate AI workloads on liquid-cooled systems powered by AMD Instinct MI350 GPUs.[ Watch replay](https://www.brighttalk.com/webcast/17278/655223)
- Abdul Dakkak, Senior Director at Modular, showcased Modular Cloud, MAX, and Mojo in a talk on Speed of Light Inference with NVIDIA and AMD GPUs, demonstrating how Modular scales AI workloads efficiently while reducing TCO.[ Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EWDG_Dxjs8)
