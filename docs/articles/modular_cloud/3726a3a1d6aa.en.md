# A New, Simpler License for MAX and Mojo

Modular Team

Product

We’re thrilled to announce a major simplification of our community license for [MAX](https://www.modular.com/max) & [Mojo](https://www.modular.com/mojo) - one that makes it easier than ever for everyone to dive in, experiment, and build great things. Over the past few months, we’ve been listening closely to the community: in [our Forum](https://forum.modular.com/), [via our Discord](https://discord.gg/modular), at Meetups, and through countless chats and emails. The feedback was clear:

- **Our license felt restrictive and confusing.** Folks weren’t sure which parts were free, which parts cost money, or how MAX and Mojo fit together.
- **Mojo should be free.** As a programming language, it shouldn’t be behind paywalls or hidden restrictions.
- **The barrier between MAX and Mojo was blurred.** People wanted a clean, easy-to-understand split.

## **A new, simpler approach**

We took this insight and just set out to simplify it. The result? A straightforward, tiered license that covers all the bases:

- **Non‑Production‑Commercial
** - **Everything is free.** Use MAX and Mojo on any device, for any research, hobby or learning project.
 - **TLDR**: No device limits. No hidden clauses. Just go build.
- **Production-Commercial on CPUs & NVIDIA
** - **Also free.** Run your live applications, internal tools, or paid services on x86 or ARM CPUs or NVIDIA GPU.
 - **TLDR**: Full access to both MAX and Mojo at zero cost.
- **Production-Commercial on Other Accelerators
** - **Free up to eight GPUs.** We don’t support other accelerators today, but we are quickly bringing up support for them. For other GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, or anything beyond NVIDIA - we allow you can scale up to eight devices without having to contact us.
 - **TLDR**: Need other compute? Simply reach out to our team to upgrade to the self‑hosted enterprise offering, or for additional licensing options.
- **Show us your success
**If you’re rocking MAX & Mojo in production for commercial use - on CPUs or NVIDIA - we just want to tell the world about your project. So we ask you to share your logo and a short story to [usage@modular.com](mailto:usage@modular.com), and we’ll spotlight your use case in our marketing, on our website, and across social media. It’s a win‑win: you get free software, and we get to brag about how amazing your product or company is.

## **It's live now, come build with us**

All the details are live right now at:

- **Pricing & FAQs:**[ https://modular.com/pricing](https://modular.com/pricing)
- **Full License Text:** [https://modular.com/legal/community](https://modular.com/legal/community)

Of course, this isn’t “set it and forget it” - we view feedback as a gift - and we want more of it! If something’s unclear, if you hit a bug, or if you simply have ideas to make MAX & Mojo even better, [jump into the forum](https://discord.gg/modular), [hit us up on Github](https://www.github.com/modular/) or email us directly.

This license update is a testament to our core philosophy: continue to build in the open, listen to the community, and keep the barrier to entry as low as we possibly can. We’re officially entering a “Build with Modular” era - whether you’re prototyping, scaling up a startup, or running mission‑critical enterprise workloads. We have a lot of exciting updates dropping across the next few months that will back up why you should Build with Modular - from more open source, to new partnerships and incredible product updates.

Here’s to the next chapter of building with Modular, MAX & Mojo. We can’t wait to see what you’ll create!
