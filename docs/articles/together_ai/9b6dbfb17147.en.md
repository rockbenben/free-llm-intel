# Together AI welcomes Alon Gavrielov as VP of Infrastructure Strategy

- AuthorsVipul Ved Prakash
- Table of contents[40+ Models Chosen for Production...40+ Models Chosen for Production...40+ Models Chosen for Production...](https://www.together.ai/blog/alon-gavrielov-as-vp-of-infrastructure-strategy#)

The rapid acceleration in AI capabilities is fueling a wave of AI-native applications that are transforming experiences across domains—from software engineering to healthcare. Many of these applications are growing exponentially, and as the adoption accelerates, we’ll see a massive demand for the infrastructure, efficient token factories that support their growth. I believe, over the next decade, we will see the *largest infrastructure buildout in human history* to these emerging AI workloads.

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, supports over 1M AI engineers and researchers building AI applications—including the fastest-growing AI-natives, such as Cursor, Decagon, and ElevenLabs. We are building AI factories from the ground up to help these applications scale rapidly and deliver a consistent experience to their exponentially growing user bases. In addition to meticulous planning for efficient power distribution, cooling, and networking, these AI factories bring together every layer of the AI stack: GPU clusters, kernels for maximum GPU performance, an optimized inference stack for best-in-class price-performance at scale, and developer services to build, shape, and serve AI-native apps. All of this is anchored in deep systems research to deliver peak performance at every layer of the stack. Delivering these highly-optimized AI factories has enabled us to deliver best-in-class results, such as [fastest inference for top open source models](https://www.together.ai/blog/fastest-inference-for-the-top-open-source-models).

As we look forward into the future and to serving our customers as they scale, we will be building AI factories from the ground up across multiple locations with Gigawatts of capacity.
As we look to the future and support our customers’ growth, we’re building AI factories from the ground up across multiple locations with gigawatts of capacity. To deliver on this vision, today I’m excited to welcome Alon Gavrielov as VP of Infrastructure Strategy. Alon brings deep experience scaling global infrastructure across key areas like capacity planning, supply chain management, business operations and data center strategy at high growth cloud and leading data center companies. Most recently at Cloudflare, he served as VP of Infrastructure, where he helped expand Cloudflare's network to hundreds of locations worldwide. During his tenure, Cloudflare’s network experienced massive growth, processing more than one hundred million requests at peak and evolving to support new products across security, application performance, storage and AI.

At Together AI, Alon will lead our infrastructure strategy and the expansion of AI factories. His team’s work across all facets of infrastructure will be essential to delivering the performance, scale, and reliability needed to support the unprecedented pace of AI adoption.

> “We are at a defining juncture where infrastructure will make or break the success of AI-native companies. I’m excited to join Together AI to build the foundation that empowers every AI team to scale seamlessly, innovate faster, and focus on delivering transformative experiences to their users.” — Alon Gavrielov, VP of Infrastructure Strategy, Together AI

Join us in welcoming Alon to the team.
