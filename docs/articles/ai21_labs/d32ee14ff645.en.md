Open-source development has reshaped AI research, but enterprise adoption still lags behind. More than [**75% of organizations** ](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/open-source-technology-in-the-age-of-ai)say they plan to expand their use of open-source AI, with many expecting those choices to reduce implementation costs. Yet, only [**13% of AI workloads**](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-mid-year-llm-market-update/) today actually run on open-source models. The gap is real, and it’s one enterprises feel every time they try to operationalize open-source systems at scale.

**This partnership exists to change that.**

Today, AI21 Labs and Together AI are bringing their strengths together to make open-source AI practical for enterprise use. Our integration combines [AI21 Maestro,](https://www.ai21.com/maestro/) our orchestration system for building reliable, multi-step AI agents, with Together AI’s high-performance open-source model platform — including native access to the full Jamba open-source family. This gives enterprises a unified, efficient way to evaluate, route, and operationalize diverse models without restructuring their workflows.

## Why this matters

As generative AI shifts from experimentation to core business operations, model choice becomes foundational. Organizations need visibility into how models behave, the ability to compare options side-by-side, and the freedom to optimize for accuracy, cost, or performance depending on the use case.

Together AI provides a powerful ecosystem of optimized open-source models. AI21 Maestro provides the orchestration layer that makes those models usable in mission-critical environments.

In the words of Vipul Ved Prakash, CEO of Together AI:

“Open-source is the way forward for AI as a platform. We believe open models democratize frontier capabilities, making them accessible to any organization and offering more control and cost efficiencies. Our collaboration with AI21 brings those models natively into enterprise environments in a seamless, actionable way.”

## A new open architecture for the enterprise

Over the past year, enterprises have made it clear: they want AI systems they can inspect, govern, and adapt with confidence. This integration delivers exactly that.

- **Together AI** provides a flexible, high-performance open-source model ecosystem.
- **AI21 Maestro** operationalizes those models with orchestration, governance, and reliable execution across complex workflows.
- **The **[**Jamba open-source family**](https://www.ai21.com/jamba/) adds efficient long-context reasoning built for enterprise-scale tasks.

With this partnership, enterprises no longer face a tradeoff between openness and operational rigor. They get the transparency and control of open-source models alongside the reliability and governance required for production.

This is what real model choice looks like. And it’s now available natively through AI21 Maestro and Together AI.
