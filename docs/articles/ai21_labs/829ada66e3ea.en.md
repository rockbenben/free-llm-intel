## TL;DR:

In his keynote at the RAISE Summit 2025, AI21 Labs co-founder and co-CEO Ori Goshen explains what it means to “AI all the work.” He breaks down why real work depends on multi-step tasks—and why LLMs alone aren’t consistent enough for enterprise use. That’s where AI21’s Maestro comes in: a system built to plan, execute, and validate complex workflows with transparency and trust.

See his full talk below.

In his talk from the [RAISE Summit](https://www.raisesummit.com/) in Paris, Ori lays out a clear path to “AI all the work.” Work, he says, is just six billion tasks waiting to be understood, broken into steps, and executed correctly. But while large language models often get single steps right—about 90% of the time—real-world tasks require multiple steps. And when you string steps together, those small errors compound. That’s why prompting alone isn’t enough for enterprises. What’s needed is something as flexible as prompting, but as robust as a hand-crafted workflow.

That’s the idea behind [Maestro](https://www.ai21.com/blog/maestro-technical-overview/). It helps developers build enterprise-grade [knowledge agents](https://www.ai21.com/blog/maestro-putting-knowledge-to-work/) that solve tasks by generating multiple plans, selecting the optimal one, and validating every step. It’s model-agnostic, allows control over budget and requirements, and returns outputs with a report card and execution graph that shows what the agent did and why.
