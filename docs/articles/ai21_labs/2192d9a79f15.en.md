## What’s SOC 2 Report?

SOC 2 is an industry-standard security framework established by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA). It evaluates an organization’s ongoing ability to maintain effective controls for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. This report requires continuous monitoring and annual validation of our security practices.
