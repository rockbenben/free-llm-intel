## Introducing AI21’s JambaCare

Introducing [AI21’s JambaCare](https://jambacare-qgomybb58qbvah3q3whys9.streamlit.app/)—your go-to solution for navigating open enrollment with ease. JambaCare empowers you to compare health insurance plans side-by-side using Jamba’s impressive 256k context window, offering deep, personalized insights. As open enrollment begins, selecting the right plan can feel overwhelming with the range of options varying by premiums, coverage, and copay structures. JambaCare, powered by AI21’s Jamba 1.5 Large model, simplifies this process by analyzing up to 800 pages of policy details in one go, enabling quick, in-depth evaluations. In this demo, JambaCare illustrates how AI-driven insights streamline plan selection to help you find the best fit for your healthcare needs and budget.

Key Features:

- **Compare and analyze:** two different insurance plans, both hundreds of pages long, at once: Select the plans you’re considering, choose from our pre-selected questions or ask it a question of your own, and Jamba will analyze the plans and return an answer based on factors like premium costs, deductibles, out-of-pocket maximums, and coverage specifics.
- **Get tailored recommendations**: Jamba tailors recommendations to your unique needs—whether you require frequent specialist visits, family coverage, or specific medication support—and provides a comparison based on the benefits that matter most to you.
- **Save time and reduce complexity**: By using Jamba’s quick, real-time analysis, you get a personalized recommendation without the hassle of combing through policy details and fine print on your own.
- **Simple and easy to build**: Running on a straightforward Jamba setup, this application effortlessly accommodates both policies in a single prompt, bypassing the need for complex Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. This simplicity ensures smooth, efficient performance.

## How JambaCare Works

Our solution shows how Jamba makes selecting a healthcare policy easy with a straightforward, user-friendly process:

- Select any two plans from the drop down of sample policies.
- Next, choose from one of our pre-selected questions or enter your own using the free text box.
- Jamba will then analyze the two selected plans, leveraging deep insights and contextual understanding to provide a clear, accurate comparison. Note: Only two plans can be compared at once to ensure precision within the model’s context window.

Here’s what Jamba will showcase:

- **In-Depth Plan Analysis**: A tailored evaluation of each plan’s features, costs, and coverage options specific to your needs.
- **Comprehensive Comparison**: A detailed, side-by-side comparison of key elements in the selected plans.**‍**
- **Cost Projections and Coverage Highlights**: Clear projections of potential costs and highlights of the most relevant coverage options for you.

## Review Your Personalized Plan Recommendations

Jamba’s analysis will yield a detailed comparison of the two plans you selected and guidance around what each plan is best suited for. With a clear and concise comparison, you can better understand which plans offer the best balance of coverage and cost for your family’s healthcare needs.
