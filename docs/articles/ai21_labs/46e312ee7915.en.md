You’ve probably seen hundreds of “agent maps” by now but most are too broad. To handle complex workflows, enterprises need to build knowledge agents. But most of today’s so-called “knowledge agents” aren’t really agents at all. They are simply retrieval-augmented chatbots, helpful for Q&A but far from what enterprises actually need.

Knowledge work is different. It is multi-step, high-stakes, and context-bound. It requires judgment, synthesis, and execution. The next frontier in AI is agents that don’t just answer, but actually do the work.

Enterprises face a trade-off. Building an AI stack provides governance and control but is resource-intensive. Buying off-the-shelf solutions speeds adoption but often locks you into rigid workflows. The winning approach will be hybrid: own the architectural spine, integrate best-in-class components, and keep critical data close to the business.

The map above highlights the players shaping this emerging stack, from evaluation and orchestration to reasoning models, retrieval layers, and packaged agents. AI21 Labs sits at the heart of this architecture, providing advanced reasoning models and orchestration capabilities that go beyond generic RAG. Our technology is designed for enterprises that need reliability, adaptability, auditability, and outcomes, not just answers. The question is not who has the biggest presence on the map, but who can help enterprises turn knowledge into action at scale.

The next wave of AI will be defined by teams that can operate: closing tickets, reconciling ledgers, and drafting proposals, all with reliable knowledge agents that can handle these complex flows.
