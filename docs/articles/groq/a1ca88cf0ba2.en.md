Today, one year this week since we launched GroqCloud, we're celebrating the one million developers who are now on GroqCloud! In just a year this community of builders, makers, and innovators have shipped incredible apps, participated in and won hackathons, productized your innovations, and even launched companies. We've also continued to welcome thousands of paid customers in our Dev Tier and companies building their AI solutions with our LPU based systems.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qfp6-OBZHA4

The moment started when we went viral this time last year. In 48 hours we had received ~3,000 API access requests, there were ~1 million prompts submitted via GroqChat, and major industry influencers were vibing with us across YouTube and X, including Matt Shumer who made some incredible GroqChat demos ranging from an [AI Answers Engine](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/1759448999457693816?s=20) to [novel writing](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/1759624554836537492?s=20) in under two minutes. Groq was also [featured on CNN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pRUddK6sxDg) with an interview of our CEO and founder, Jonathan Ross.

We’ll see you even more in the coming year across the US, Middle East, South Asia, the EU, and online. This last year showed us many inspired builds so we can’t wait to see what engineers cook up next with our fast AI inference solutions. We'll continue innovating to provide you the very best.

As always, entries to our [Groq API Cookbook](https://github.com/groq/groq-api-cookbook/tree/main/tutorials/jigsawstack-prompt-engine) are welcome and appreciated! This live repo, powered by our open-source community, continues to aggregate great tips, cool apps, and informative demos that we are proud to share with everyone.

And don’t forget that we launched self-serve access to Dev Tier – this means you can keep pushing your builds, apps, and solutions to the max with higher rate limits and more service options like Flex Pricing and Batch Processing. Share your projects in our [Discord community](https://discord.com/invite/groq) of 22K+ members and tag us on your social media posts so we can comment, like, and share.

Thanks for helping us learn about your experiences, and iterating on our products and services; teaching us about what's possible when we enable the creativity of developers with fast inference and ultra-low latency. Thank you again for making this past year one for the record books, we wouldn’t be here without all of you. We’ll keep innovating on inference, you keep shipping, and together we will both **Build Fast!**
