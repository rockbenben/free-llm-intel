# Introducing Grok Business and Grok Enterprise

The best assistant in the world is now Enterprise ready.

## [Bring Grok to your team with seamless security and speed](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#bring-grok-to-your-team-with-seamless-security-and-speed)

We're putting Grok in the hands of employees everywhere, with enterprise security and privacy built in from the ground up. **Your data stays yours: no training on it, *ever***. You get the highest rate limits on our strongest models, all in a single platform your team can share.

For small-to-medium teams, we offer a self-serve process to use **Grok Business**. To support larger organizations, we also offer **Grok Enterprise** which includes everything in Grok Business plus Custom SSO, Directory Sync (SCIM), and more for seamless organizational management.

## [Real business outcomes, built together](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#real-business-outcomes-built-together)

For end-users, Grok Business makes secure AI work feel effortless. You can pull in data from your company's tools like Google Drive and share insights safely with teammates. Links are only accessible by those you share them with.







Share conversations securely with your teammates. Links are only accessible to the people you share them with.

### [Leverage your company knowledge with Grok](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#leverage-your-company-knowledge-with-grok)

Grok has the ability to access your company knowledge and tools directly with **Apps**. Starting with Google Drive, your team is guaranteed:

- **Permission-awareness by design.** Grok respects your existing Google Drive permissions. If a file isn't shared with you in Drive, you won't see it in Grok.
- **Citations you can trust.** Every answer includes citations linking directly to source documents, with quote previews and highlighted relevant sections.

Grok can also perform agentic search using our industry-leading [Collections API](https://x.ai/news/grok-collections-api) via **Projects**. This is useful for when Grok needs to use a large document store as a primary source, such as a data room for analyzing legal documentation or building financial models.







Bring your company tools into Grok for agentic tasks, starting with searching your Google Drive documents

## [Streamline administration with a single, unified platform](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#streamline-administration-with-a-single-unified-platform)

Manage your Grok Business team directly in the xAI console for effortless administration. Invite users, manage access controls, and monitor usage, all in one place.










Monitor your team's usage in real-time to validate efficacy of the platform.

## [Scaling up](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#scaling-up)

### [Enterprise tier](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#enterprise-tier)

Grok Business is designed to support the smallest businesses to the largest Enterprises. With our Enterprise tier, customers have access to:

- Custom Single Sign On (SSO)
- Directory Sync (SCIM)
- Advanced audit and security controls
- *[and more](https://x.ai/grok/business)*

### [Ultimate privacy with Enterprise Vault](https://x.ai/news/grok-business#ultimate-privacy-with-enterprise-vault)

Vault enables our most secure Enterprise customers to use grok.com for all business-critical applications. Not only is data encrypted in transit and at rest, but it's encrypted with *your keys*, under your control, and isolated from all other customers. Vault supports:

- Dedicated data plane
- Application-level encryption
- Customer managed encryption keys (CMEK)
