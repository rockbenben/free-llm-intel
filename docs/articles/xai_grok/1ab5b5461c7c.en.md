# Supporting the DOW's mission with AI

xAI is proud to be selected by the US Department of War to deliver Frontier AI

## [xAI selected by the US Department of War to power Enterprise AI and Mission Systems](https://x.ai/news/us-gov-dept-of-war#xai-selected-by-the-us-department-of-war-to-power-enterprise-ai-and-mission-systems)

xAI is proud to announce that its suite of Frontier AI systems specially designed for United States Military and National Security users, was selected by the U.S. Department of War (DOW) for Frontier AI use cases as a part of DOW’s GenAI.Mil suite. GenAI.mil will enable 3 million military and civilian employees of the DOW to access xAI technologies at DOW Impact Level 5 (DOW IL5).

This new partnership between xAI and the DoW’s Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) brings xAI’s Frontier AI systems, powered by the Grok family of models, to both Enterprise AI and critical mission use cases.

xAI for Government is an AI platform designed for Enterprise use cases across federal, state, and local government agencies in the United States. The platform combines access to xAI’s industry-leading AI models, agentic tools, research platform, and API. xAI Government users within the DoW will also have unique access to real-time global insights from the X platform. When made generally available at IL5, xAI for Government can be embedded into the day-to-day workflows of all 3 million military and civilian employees of the DoW, from the Pentagon to the tactical edge.

In addition to Enterprise use cases, xAI is bringing the power of Frontier AI and real-time insights directly to the warfighter. Through an ongoing, long-term partnership with the DoW and other mission partners, xAI will make available a family of government-optimized foundation models to support classified operational workloads.

These two new partnerships are part of our longstanding support of the United States Government and xAI’s mission to bring the best tools and technologies available in industry to benefit our nation.
