# Grok Imagine Video 1.5

Improved quality at even faster speeds.

Try it on Grok

Try it on API

Grok Imagine Video 1.5 is now generally available on the [Imagine API](https://console.x.ai/team/default/video?flow=explore&campaign=imagine-video-1-5-blog&utm_source=website&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=imagine-video-1-5-blog). We've also rolled out Video 1.5 Fast on [grok.com/imagine](https://grok.com/imagine?referrer=website&campaign=imagine-video-1-5-blog) and our [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/grok-ai-chat-video/id6670324846) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.x.grok) apps. These are our best image-to-video models yet: better motion, better physics, better audio, at the fastest speeds. We’re also adding features to enhance your creative workflow in Grok Imagine.

Compared to the previous model, 1.5 improves across every dimension that matters for real creative work:

## [Audio and speech](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#audio-and-speech)

Sound effects, ambience, and dialogue are generated in the same pass and land on the action. Speech is clearer and better synced.

## [Motion and physics](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#motion-and-physics)

Movement holds together over the length of a clip — fewer warps, more believable weight and momentum.

## [Speed](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#speed)

Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Fast almost doubles generation speed: it produces 6-second, 720p videos in about 25 seconds, down from 40+ seconds in our previous model.

## [Enhanced productivity](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#enhanced-productivity)

### [Projects](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#projects)

## [Now out of preview in the API](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-video-1-5#now-out-of-preview-in-the-api)

Imagine Video 1.5 is out of preview and generally available in the [xAI API](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/video/generation) as `grok-imagine-video-1.5`. Give it a starting image, describe the motion, and choose your resolution and duration.

```
import os
import xai_sdk

client = xai_sdk.Client(api_key=os.getenv("XAI_API_KEY"))

response = client.video.generate(
    prompt="Slow cinematic push-in as embers drift across the battlefield and the helmet's crest stirs in the wind",
    model="grok-imagine-video-1.5",
    image_url="https://your-host.com/helmet.jpg",
    duration=10,
    resolution="720p",
)

print(response.url)
```
