# Use Grok in Warp

Use your Grok and X Premium subscription inside Warp.

You can now use your Grok or X Premium subscription inside [Warp](https://www.warp.dev/), an agentic development environment built on the terminal and used by almost one million developers.

Your existing subscription provides access to Grok models, including `grok-build-0.1`, the model powering [Grok Build](https://x.ai/build).

## [Setup](https://x.ai/news/grok-warp#setup)

- Download Warp from [warp.dev/download](https://www.warp.dev/download) and open it.
- Open the "Agent > Warp Agent" settings page and click "Connect SuperGrok subscription"
- Switch to the `grok-build-0.1` model and start prompting.

That's it! More agents and integrations are coming soon to SuperGrok and X Premium subscribers. To subscribe, visit the [pricing page](https://x.ai/pricing).
