### [[New Model Release] OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Image Now Available](https://docs.siliconflow.cn/docs/release-notes/overview#new-model-release-openmoss-teammoss-image-now-available)

The image generation model `OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Image` is now available on the platform, launched on **2026-11-03**. It is suited to posters, infographics, illustrations, and photorealistic image creation.

The model is billed per image at **¥0.30 / image**. Call it through `/v1/images/generations`; see [Image Generation](https://docs.siliconflow.cn/docs/userguide/capabilities/images) for usage. For the complete list of available models, see [Models](https://cloud.siliconflow.cn/models?types=to-image).
