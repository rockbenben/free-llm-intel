- We’ve added two event types to [organization webhooks](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/organization-webhooks#encryption-key-events), which are in beta. We send `external_key.failed` when your key management service keeps refusing a customer-managed encryption key, and `external_key.recovered` when the key works again.
