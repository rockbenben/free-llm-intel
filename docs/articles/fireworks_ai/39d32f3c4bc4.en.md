[Hy4 Flash](https://fireworks.ai/models/fireworks/hy4-flash), Tencent’s new open weights model, is now available on Fireworks serverless. It’s a mixture-of-experts model with about 220B total parameters and about 12B active per token, a 1M-token context window (512K on Fireworks at launch), and text and image input. Tencent built it for document processing, research, office work and agent workflows.

Hy4 Flash is on the score-cost Pareto frontier for every [Specialized Intelligence Index](https://fireworks.ai/specialized-intelligence-index/) benchmark where we recorded cost. On those boards, no model scores higher for less. The clearest example is Epiq’s CorporateBench, where it edged out Claude Opus 5.5 at over 200x less per task ($0.013 vs $2.80). It also tied Claude Opus 5 on Rogo’s Big Finance Benchmark and matched it on Sierra’s τ³-Banking, again for over 200x less.

Hy4 Flash runs on Fireworks serverless at $0.07 per million uncached input tokens, $0.003 per million cached input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens on the Standard tier, with a Priority tier also available. It’s served globally.

## Hy4 Flash keeps pace with Claude Opus on professional work

We ran Hy4 Flash on seven [Specialized Intelligence Index](https://fireworks.ai/specialized-intelligence-index/) benchmarks, using the same harness as every other model on each board, at high reasoning. Cost is the recorded tokens at Fireworks serverless prices. Three results stand out:

- Ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 on **Epiq’s CorporateBench**. This benchmark tests answering questions across thousands of internal company emails and meeting notes. Hy4 Flash scored 75.9%, 6th of 14 models and just above Claude Opus 5.5 (75.4%). It cost $0.013 a task to Opus 5.5’s $2.80, over 200x less, and was the lowest-cost model on the board.
- Tied with Claude Opus 5 on **Rogo’s Big Finance Benchmark**. Both scored 49.2%, but Hy4 Flash cost $0.0065 a task and Opus 5 cost $1.83, about 280x more. Hy4 Flash also finished ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 and Kimi K3.1 Pro (48.2% each). The only model on the board that costs less, GPT-6 Luna, scored 35.3%.
- Level with Claude Opus 5 on **Sierra’s τ³-Banking**. On this agent benchmark for banking tasks, Hy4 Flash scored 49.2% to Opus 5’s 49.1%, at $0.012 a trial against $2.59. It’s the lowest-cost model on the board, which puts it on the board’s cost frontier.

Hy4 Flash was the lowest-cost model on every board where we recorded cost, except Big Finance. It ranks lower on HealthBench Professional (16th of 18) and DeepSWE (19th of 22), and for long-horizon software engineering Tencent recommends the larger Hy4.

*Results are from Fireworks’ [Specialized Intelligence Index](https://fireworks.ai/specialized-intelligence-index/) runs on the Fireworks deployment of Hy4 Flash, as of November 22, 2026.*
