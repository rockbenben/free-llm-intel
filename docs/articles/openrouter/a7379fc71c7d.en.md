# December Release Spotlight

OpenRouter ·12/18/2025 · Updated 6/24/2026

Our monthly Product Spotlight highlights a few of our biggest releases from the past month.

## Claude Code on OpenRouter

You can now use OpenRouter with Claude Code, allowing you to track your team’s usage and control your costs.

[Try It](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/guides/claude-code-integration) | [Announcement](https://x.com/OpenRouterAI/status/2002067582569066979)

## Response Healing: automatic JSON repair

LLMs break JSON more than you’d think. OpenRouter now automatically fixes malformed responses (trailing commas, unescaped characters, missing brackets) before they reach your app. 80% defect reduction on Gemini 2.0 Flash, 99.8% on Qwen3 235B. Free, non-streaming only, and <1ms overhead. Enable it under Settings > Plugins.

[Announcement](https://openrouter.ai/announcements/response-healing-reduce-json-defects-by-80percent)

## Chatroom notifications
