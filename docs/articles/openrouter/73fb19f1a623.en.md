# Deep Research & Many New Models

OpenRouter ·3/6/2025 · Updated 6/11/2026

## New Sonar Models, Including Deep Research, Now Available:

- [**Sonar Deep Research**](https://openrouter.ai/perplexity/sonar-deep-research): Engineered explicitly for comprehensive research tasks, Sonar Deep Research automates in-depth searches, iteratively evaluates sources, and synthesizes structured analyses for complex subjects across multiple domains.
- [**Sonar Reasoning Pro**](https://openrouter.ai/perplexity/sonar-reasoning-pro): Advanced reasoning capabilities powered by DeepSeek R1 with Chain of Thought (CoT). Perfect for sophisticated queries requiring detailed analysis and extensive sourcing.
- [**Sonar Pro**](https://openrouter.ai/perplexity/sonar-pro): Enhanced model capable of handling complex, multi-step queries with a larger context window, ideal for detailed searches and nuanced follow-ups.

**Note:** Pricing for these models includes additional charges based on Perplexity’s internal search and reasoning steps. Full pricing details are available [here](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/pricing).

## More New Models on OpenRouter:

### Creative & Interactive Storytelling:

- [**Skyfall 36B v2**](https://openrouter.ai/thedrummer/skyfall-36b-v2): Fine-tuned from Mistral Small for enhanced creativity, nuanced storytelling, and immersive roleplay.
- [**Wayfarer Large 70B**](https://openrouter.ai/latitudegames/wayfarer-large-70b-llama-3.3): Optimized for realistic, narrative-rich interactive fiction with meaningful consequences.
- [**Anubis Pro 105B v1**](https://openrouter.ai/thedrummer/anubis-pro-105b-v1): Expanded variant of Llama 3.3 70B with deeper character portrayals, emotional intelligence, and coherent storytelling.

### Advanced & Efficient:

- [**Reka Flash 3**](https://openrouter.ai/rekaai/reka-flash-3:free): A 21B-parameter model optimized for efficient general chat, coding, reasoning, and function-calling tasks with explicit internal reasoning tags.
- [**Cohere Command A**](https://openrouter.ai/cohere/command-a): 111B parameters with a 256K context window for multilingual, coding, and agentic tasks.
- [**Jamba 1.6 Large**](https://openrouter.ai/ai21/jamba-1.6-large): 94B active parameters, multilingual proficiency, structured JSON outputs, and tool-use capabilities.
- [**Jamba 1.6 Mini**](https://openrouter.ai/ai21/jamba-1.6-mini): Compact 12B parameter model, multilingual, structured outputs, and efficient tool use.
