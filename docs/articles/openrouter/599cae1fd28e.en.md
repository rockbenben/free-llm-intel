# Introducing Cloudflare as a new provider

OpenRouter ·2/4/2025 · Updated 6/11/2026

## **Cloudflare models are now available on OpenRouter** 🌩️

Cloudflare’s Workers AI platform is now serving a range of open-source models on OpenRouter, offering low-latency access and competitive pricing.

- [**Llama 3.3 70B**](https://openrouter.ai/meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct)
