# Presets: How To Seamlessly Transfer Model Configurations Across Apps

OpenRouter ·7/29/2025 · Updated 6/11/2026

Presets are a powerful new way to easily transfer your model configurations between apps. Now you can set up your favorite settings once and reuse them anywhere, saving you time and effort.

## Step 1: Create a Preset

Visit [https://openrouter.ai/settings/presets](https://openrouter.ai/settings/presets) and click **Add Preset**.

![Preview 1](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/017cd6e3-8463-4c19-d933-736df9459300/public)

## Step 2: Configure your settings

You can customize:

- default model
- system prompt
- provider preferences
- default sampling parameters (like temperature), and more

![Preview 2](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/32bdd3b8-9ab3-45f7-a029-dbb5cafe1900/public)

## Step 3: Test the preset

Click **Test in Chat** and confirm it works the way you want.

![Preview 3](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/53b618e9-7600-4b9c-c645-4c958f5e7700/public)

## Step 4: Use it in your apps

Set the preset as your model in any app that supports bring your own model.
 You can also select the underlying model with the new `preset` field or `model@preset`.

![Preview 4](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/965229c1-ebfc-43ae-e1ac-6f8c8f1a8200/public)

## Step 5: Roll back any time

Want to go back to a previous version? Use the version history to roll back easily.

![Preview 5](https://openrouter.ai/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/Xq3eUzdKO2-MoBklzEEuMQ/04eab7ed-c652-4485-be30-571a2c7a6c00/public)
