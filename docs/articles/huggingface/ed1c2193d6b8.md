## 什么是重排器，为什么要把它和嵌入模型配对？

重排器（又称 pointwise cross-encoder）是一类神经网络模型，输入一个 `(query, document)` 对，输出一个相关性分数。与嵌入模型分别编码查询和文档、再从两个向量算相似度不同，重排器让两段文本在每一个 transformer 层里互相做注意力。这种联合编码更准，但也更贵：模型必须对每个 `(query, document)` 对各跑一遍，而不是每段文本各跑一遍。

由于 cross-encoder 在整个语料上跑代价太高，业界通用的生产模式是**先检索再重排（retrieve-then-rerank）**：先用一个快速嵌入模型检索出 top-K 候选（便宜），再用 cross-encoder 对这 K 个做高精度重排序。总开销保持可控，而最终排序非常接近"穷举式 cross-encoder 全量打分"的结果。

[![Embedding vs Reranker Models](https://huggingface.co/datasets/huggingface/documentation-images/resolve/main/blog/train-reranker/embedding_vs_reranker_model.png)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/huggingface/documentation-images/resolve/main/blog/train-reranker/embedding_vs_reranker_model.png)

> 全文中"reranker（重排器）"和"cross-encoder"将交替使用。

## 用法

发布的模型都是普通的 Sentence Transformers `CrossEncoder` 模型，三行代码即可使用：

```
from sentence_transformers import CrossEncoder

model = CrossEncoder("cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1")
scores = model.predict([
    ("Where was Apple founded?", "Apple Inc. was founded in Cupertino, California in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne."),
    ("Where was Apple founded?", "The Fuji apple is an apple cultivar developed in the late 1930s and brought to market in 1962."),
])
print(scores)
# [11.393298  2.968891]   <- larger means more relevant
```

对于一个查询和一组候选，也可以用 `rank` 拿到排序后的索引和分数：

```
ranked = model.rank(
    query="Which planet is known as the Red Planet?",
    documents=[
        "Venus is often called Earth's twin because of its similar size and proximity.",
        "Mars, known for its reddish appearance, is often referred to as the Red Planet.",
        "Jupiter, the largest planet in our solar system, has a prominent red spot.",
        "Saturn, famous for its rings, is sometimes mistaken for the Red Planet.",
    ],
    top_k=4,
    return_documents=True,
)
for r in ranked:
    print(f"({r['score']:.2f}): {r['text']}")
# (10.82): Mars, known for its reddish appearance, is often referred to as the Red Planet.
# (9.86): Saturn, famous for its rings, is sometimes mistaken for the Red Planet.
# (8.55): Jupiter, the largest planet in our solar system, has a prominent red spot.
# (6.21): Venus is often called Earth's twin because of its similar size and proximity.
```

把 [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1) 换成其他尺寸，就能在质量与速度之间做取舍。得益于 ModernBERT 的长上下文预训练，六个模型全部支持最长 8K token 的上下文（对长文档重排很有用）。

建议安装 [`kernels`](https://github.com/huggingface/kernels) 并设置 `model_kwargs={"dtype": "bfloat16", "attn_implementation": "flash_attention_2"}` 以获得最高吞吐。细节见下文[速度](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#speed)一节，一般来说，相比默认加载可获得 1.7 倍到 8.3 倍提速，具体取决于模型尺寸和序列长度。

```
from sentence_transformers import CrossEncoder

model = CrossEncoder(
    "cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1",
    model_kwargs={"dtype": "bfloat16", "attn_implementation": "flash_attention_2"},
)
```

### 端到端的检索-重排流水线

一个完整示例：用快速嵌入模型做检索、用重排器做最终排序：

```
from sentence_transformers import SentenceTransformer, CrossEncoder

# Fast retrieval with a static embedder (sub-millisecond on CPU per query)
embedder = SentenceTransformer("sentence-transformers/static-retrieval-mrl-en-v1")
reranker = CrossEncoder("cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-68m-v1")

corpus = [
    "Apple Inc. was founded in Cupertino, California in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.",
    "The Fuji apple is an apple cultivar developed in the late 1930s.",
    "Steve Jobs introduced the iPhone in 2007 at Macworld.",
    "Macintosh computers were sold by Apple from 1984 onward.",
    # ... thousands or millions more in production
]
query = "Where was Apple founded?"

# Step 1: encode + retrieve top-100
query_emb = embedder.encode_query(query, convert_to_tensor=True)
corpus_emb = embedder.encode_document(corpus, convert_to_tensor=True)
scores = embedder.similarity(query_emb, corpus_emb)[0]
top_k_idx = scores.topk(min(100, len(corpus))).indices.tolist()

# Step 2: rerank
top_k_docs = [corpus[i] for i in top_k_idx]
ranked = reranker.rank(query, top_k_docs, top_k=5, return_documents=True)
for r in ranked:
    print(f"({r['score']:.2f}): {r['text']}")
# (11.63): Apple Inc. was founded in Cupertino, California in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.
# (4.71): Steve Jobs introduced the iPhone in 2007 at Macworld.
# (1.96): The Fuji apple is an apple cultivar developed in the late 1930s.
# (1.49): Macintosh computers were sold by Apple from 1984 onward.
```

这正是大多数现代搜索系统采用的结构。检索器决定什么能进入漏斗，重排器决定谁排在最前。

## 架构细节

六个重排器架构完全相同，只差骨干模型的大小。骨干来自 Johns Hopkins 大学 Ettin 系列中的六个 [Ettin 编码器](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin)之一。它们是 ModernBERT 风格的模型，采用去填充注意力（unpadded attention）、RoPE 位置编码、GeGLU，在 2T token 的开放许可语料上预训练，支持最长 8192 token 上下文。

在每个编码器之上，重排器使用一个 4 模块分类头，复刻 `ModernBertForSequenceClassification` 的结构，但由 Sentence Transformers 的模块化组件搭建。底层 `Transformer` 加载的是普通 `AutoModel` 而非 `AutoModelForSequenceClassification`，从而能对变长输入做序列去填充（unpadding）以配合 Flash Attention 2。在中等文档长度下，相比 fp32+SDPA 可获得 1.7x–8.3x 提速，具体取决于模型大小（完整基准见[速度](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#speed)）：

```
1. Transformer(FA2)
2. Pooling(cls)
3. Dense(H, H, bias=False, GELU)
4. LayerNorm(H)
5. Dense(H, 1, scores)
```

在我的消融实验里，CLS 池化优于均值池化。这有点出乎意料：ModernBERT 只有每三层才有一次全局注意力，其余三分之二是局部窗口注意力，远距离位置的 token 根本"够不到" CLS。但经验上，那几个全局层携带的信号足以让 CLS 成为更好的池化选择。

### 速度

质量数字只是重排器的一半，另一半是它的延迟能否塞进检索与展示结果之间的预算。下面讲讲我测了什么。

我在单张 NVIDIA H100 80GB 上，把六个发布模型与 13 个公开重排器（参数量最高约 1B 的强基线）做了对比基准。查询和文档来自 [`sentence-transformers/natural-questions`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sentence-transformers/natural-questions)，保持其自然文档长度分布：NQ 答案大多很短、少数很长。文档在 `max_length=512` 处截断，以免给老模型不公平的优势。每个模型都用其支持的最佳注意力实现：凡架构允许就上 Flash Attention 2（BERT、XLM-RoBERTa、ModernBERT、Qwen2），不支持则用 SDPA，DeBERTa-v2 用 eager（`transformers` 目前既不支持它的 FA2 也不支持 SDPA）。

对每个模型，自动批量搜索从 batch size 8 起步，不断翻倍直到 GPU 显存耗尽。每个 batch size 跑三次计时、取中位吞吐，避免单次失手拖累数字。报告的吞吐取表现最好的那个 batch size。

我们的 150M 的对照更有意思，因为恰好在 150M 参数量上有两个直接的同架构对手：[`Alibaba-NLP/gte-reranker-modernbert-base`](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-NLP/gte-reranker-modernbert-base) 和 [`ibm-granite/granite-embedding-reranker-english-r2`](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-embedding-reranker-english-r2)。二者都构建在同一 ModernBERT-base 骨干上。我们的 150M 跑到每秒 3237 对，两个对手分别只有 1418 和 1404，相差 2.3 倍。

三个 150M 模型都用了 Flash Attention 2，但两个对手经由 `AutoModelForSequenceClassification` 加载，输入保持填充（padded）。也就是说注意力确实跑了 FA2 内核，但模型其余部分仍在对毫无贡献的 padding token 做稠密计算。我们的模块化 `Transformer` 模块（见上文[架构细节](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#architecture-details)）让去填充输入贯穿整个模型，每一层只在真实 token 上花算力。这就是"只吃到 FA2 部分收益"和"吃满全部收益"的区别。

再看表格底部：我们的 1B 达到每秒 928 对，比 1.54B 的教师 `mxbai-rerank-large-v2`（387 对/秒）快 2.4 倍，同时 MTEB 分数与它只差 0.0001。教师基于 Qwen2，每对查询-文档都带提示模板的额外开销，而蒸馏出的学生继承了教师的标定和判断，却甩掉了这些运行时包袱。说实话，这是整个发布里我最满意的单个数字。

一个遗憾：基于 DeBERTa-v2 的 `mxbai-rerank-{xsmall,base,large}-v1` 系列在表中明显更慢，因为 DeBERTa-v2 目前在 `transformers` 里既不支持 Flash Attention 2 也不支持 SDPA。70M 的 `mxbai-rerank-xsmall-v1` 只有每秒 2636 对，约为参数量几乎相同的我们 68M（4913）的一半。模型本身完全没问题，只是用不上现代注意力内核。

| [`mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-base-v2`](https://huggingface.co/mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-base-v2) | 494M | SDPA | 3.5 |
| **[`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1)** | **1B** | SDPA | **2.1** |

在 CPU 上用不上 bf16、Flash Attention 2 或去填充，延迟的故事就简单多了：参数量越大越慢。17M 模型显著快于 `ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2`（267.4 vs 143.9 对/秒），甚至比更小的 `ms-marco-MiniLM-L4-v2`（206.2）还快。如预期，我们的 150M 这回与两个 150M 对手并驾齐驱（14.0 vs 14.5 和 14.7 对/秒），因为去填充优势不存在了。如果你是 CPU 场景，我们的 17M 和 32M 是务实之选。

为了解释速度从何而来，下表用相同基准配置，对我们的六个模型逐一扫描 `fp32+SDPA`、`bf16+SDPA` 和 `bf16+FA2`。FA2 列分两种：输入仍带 padding 的（包装式模型会遇到的情况）和去填充输入的（我们的模块化 `Transformer` 实际的做法）。最右列是 FA2 开启时我们模型的默认配置。

**表 2.** 六个发布尺寸在 `max_length=512`、自然 NQ 文档上的精度与注意力消融。每个单元格显示对/秒，括号里是相对 `fp32+SDPA` 的倍数，第二行为峰值 GPU 显存。最右列（**粗体**）是 FA2 开启时我们模型默认使用的配置。

| 模型 | 参数量 | fp32+SDPA | bf16+SDPA | bf16+FA2 带 padding | **bf16+FA2 去 padding** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`ettin-reranker-17m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-17m-v1) | 17M | 4402 (1.00x) 0.8 GB | 4523 (1.03x) 2.2 GB | 3744 (0.85x) 1.9 GB | **7517 (1.71x)** **1.4 GB** |
| [`ettin-reranker-32m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1) | 32M | 3307 (1.00x) 1.2 GB | 4357 (1.32x) 1.6 GB | 3040 (0.92x) 2.9 GB | **6602 (2.00x)** **1.1 GB** |
| [`ettin-reranker-68m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-68m-v1) | 68M | 1364 (1.00x) 1.0 GB | 2861 (2.10x) 2.2 GB | 2003 (1.47x) 2.0 GB | **4913 (3.60x)** **1.5 GB** |
| [`ettin-reranker-150m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-150m-v1) | 150M | 671 (1.00x) 1.6 GB | 1942 (2.90x) 1.8 GB | 1396 (2.08x) 3.1 GB | **3237 (4.83x)** **1.4 GB** |
| [`ettin-reranker-400m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-400m-v1) | 400M | 266 (1.00x) 2.5 GB | 1113 (4.18x) 1.8 GB | 864 (3.25x) 2.7 GB | **1738 (6.53x)** **2.2 GB** |
| [`ettin-reranker-1b-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1) | 1B | 112 (1.00x) 4.6 GB | 630 (5.60x) 2.8 GB | 522 (4.64x) 3.6 GB | **928 (8.26x)** **4.5 GB** |

从 `fp32+SDPA` 基线到 `bf16+FA2 去 padding` 的总提速随模型规模急剧增长，从 17M 的 1.71x 到 1B 的 8.26x。增长的大头来自 `bf16` 本身：从 `fp32+SDPA` 到 `bf16+SDPA` 这一步，17M 只提速 1.03x，而 1B 直接 5.60x——这部分也因为显存占用降低允许更大的 batch。简而言之，`bfloat16` 是总提速的最大单一贡献者。

出人意料的是，在输入仍带 padding 的情况下打开 FA2，发布的全部尺寸上都反而比 `bf16+SDPA` 更慢。FA2 内核偏爱去填充格式，喂给它带 padding 的输入，你既要付出格式转换的簿记开销，又仍在 padding token 上浪费算力。所以 `bf16+FA2 带 padding` 列大致相当于：只在 `model_kwargs` 里把 `sdpa` 换成 `flash_attention_2`、其他都不改时你测到的数字。表 1 里的 `gte-reranker-modernbert-base` 和 `granite-embedding-reranker-english-r2` 就处于这种状态。

最后，从 `bf16+FA2 带 padding` 到 `bf16+FA2 去 padding` 还能再带来 1.78x（1B）到 2.45x（68M）的额外吞吐，同时大幅降低峰值显存，从而允许更大的 batch。

所以我的建议很简单：`bf16` 和 FA2 一起打开。六个 Ettin 重排器默认就会使用去填充输入，因为[架构细节](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#architecture-details)一节的模块化 `Transformer` 就是按这个配置的。完整代码片段与[用法](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#usage)一节相同：

```
from sentence_transformers import CrossEncoder

model = CrossEncoder(
    "cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-150m-v1",
    model_kwargs={
        "dtype": "bfloat16",
        "attn_implementation": "flash_attention_2",  # See tip below
    },
)
```

> 用 pip install kernels 安装 FA2。它为各种 GPU 架构、CUDA 版本和操作系统提供了预编译内核。

给其他 CrossEncoder 提个醒：这份完整提速只有像 Ettin 重排器这样用模块化 `Transformer` 构建的模型才吃得到。对一个经由 `AutoModelForSequenceClassification` 加载的 CrossEncoder 套用同样两个开关，你会落在表 2 中更慢的 `bf16+FA2 带 padding` 列。

## 训练

下面的训练脚本最初是新发布的 [`train-sentence-transformers` Agent Skill](https://github.com/huggingface/sentence-transformers/tree/main/skills) 的产物，该 Skill 随 [Sentence Transformers v5.5.0](https://github.com/huggingface/sentence-transformers/releases/tag/v5.5.0) 发布。如果你使用 AI 编程智能体（Claude Code、Codex、Cursor、Gemini CLI……），安装这个 Skill 后就可以让它在你的数据上微调 `SentenceTransformer`、`CrossEncoder` 或 `SparseEncoder` 模型。该 Skill 内置了版本感知的指导，覆盖基座模型选择、损失函数与评估器选择、困难负样本挖掘、蒸馏、LoRA、Matryoshka、多语言训练和静态嵌入，并为每种模型类型提供模板脚本。

```
hf skills add train-sentence-transformers --claude   # symlinks into .claude/skills/
hf skills add train-sentence-transformers --global   # under ~/.agents/skills/
```

像 *"在我的数据集上针对 (query, document) 对微调一个 cross-encoder 重排器，挖掘困难负样本，并推送到我的 Hub 仓库"* 这样的提示词，就能生成一个可运行的脚本，之后你再持续迭代。我就是这样开始打磨下面这套配方的。

六个重排器全部使用相同的单阶段配方训练，只有学习率和每设备 batch size 随模型尺寸变化。完整训练脚本约 150 行，只用一个已发布数据集。

这套配方只跨模型尺寸扫了一轮就收敛了。每个尺寸的学习率用最终训练数据约 15% 的子集做了小规模网格搜索，所得学习率无需重调即可直接平移到全量数据训练。除了学习率，各尺寸不需要其他单独调参。

### 蒸馏配方

多数公开的重排器配方在人工标注的相关性三元组（一个查询、一个正文档，可选困难负样本）上训练，使用 [`MultipleNegativesRankingLoss`](https://sbert.net/docs/package_reference/cross_encoder/losses.html#multiplenegativesrankingloss)、[`BinaryCrossEntropyLoss`](https://sbert.net/docs/package_reference/cross_encoder/losses.html#binarycrossentropyloss)、[`RankNetLoss`](https://sbert.net/docs/package_reference/cross_encoder/losses.html#ranknetloss) 或 [`LambdaLoss`](https://sbert.net/docs/package_reference/cross_encoder/losses.html#lambdaloss) 这类对比式、pointwise、pairwise 或 listwise 损失。可参见我此前的 [Training and Finetuning Reranker Models with Sentence Transformers](https://huggingface.co/blog/train-reranker) 博客。

但这条路有几个实际和理论上的短板。第一，正样本需要人工标注，跨多领域既贵又慢。第二，模型只见过别人人工过的那一小部分 `(query, document)` 对的标签；尤其经过困难负样本挖掘后，会剩下大量假负样本，正如 [Hard Negatives, Hard Lessons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16967) 所展示的。第三，这种二值标注与现实不符——有些文档就是比其他文档更相关。

我这里选了另一条路：从一个已有的强教师重排器做 pointwise MSE 蒸馏。设置简单到三行就能说清：

- **教师**：[`mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-large-v2`](https://huggingface.co/mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-large-v2)（1.54B 参数）。
- **损失**：对教师原始 logits（范围约 [−12, 22]）直接做 [`MSELoss`](https://sbert.net/docs/package_reference/cross_encoder/losses.html#mseloss)，不做缩放。
- **训练数据**：约 1.43 亿条 `(query, document, teacher_score)` 三元组。

### 数据集

我把训练数据作为单个 Hugging Face 数据集发布：[`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data)，由两个来源拼装而成，每个来源保留为独立 split，让数据来源透明可溯：

- LightOn 预训练数据（[`lightonai/embeddings-pre-training`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-pre-training)，未经筛选）：32 个 split，覆盖广泛领域的文本相似性信号（MTP、FW-EDU、Reddit、PAQ、S2ORC、Amazon、Wikipedia、MS MARCO 等）。我对部分 split 限制了样本量，合计约 1.1 亿条 `(query, document, similarity)` 三元组。
- 来自 [`lightonai/embeddings-fine-tuning`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-fine-tuning) 的重新打分检索数据：7 个 split（`msmarco`、`hotpotqa`、`trivia`、`nq`、`squadv2`、`fiqa`、`fever`）。源数据集每个查询最多有 2048 个候选文档（最初用 [`Alibaba-NLP/gte-modernbert-base`](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-NLP/gte-modernbert-base) 打分），我用 [`mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-large-v2`](https://huggingface.co/mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-large-v2) 重新打分并上传为 [`cross-encoder/lightonai-embeddings-fine-tuning-reranked-v1`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/lightonai-embeddings-fine-tuning-reranked-v1)。该数据集用 [Jang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04734) 的分位数锚点配方把每个查询的 2048 个候选下采样到 256（全部正样本 + top-16 困难负样本 + 约 239 个分位数锚点分层样本）。训练时我再从这 256 个中每个查询取 64 个：按分数排序头部取 32 个（正样本加上最难的负样本），另 32 个中等难度负样本从教师排序更靠后的一个区间里采样。具体 rank 位置见[数据集卡片](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data)。

合计：约 1.43 亿条 `(query, document, score)` 三元组，另有一个 5K 行的保留评估 split（`quora` 的尾部数据），用于训练中的评估损失。

### 训练参数

多数超参数在所有模型尺寸间保持一致：

```
CrossEncoderTrainingArguments(
    num_train_epochs=1,                    # I chose more data over more epochs
    per_device_train_batch_size=...,       # global_batch_size // world_size (see table below)
    gradient_accumulation_steps=1,
    learning_rate=...,                     # per-size, see table
    warmup_ratio=0.03,                     # ~3% linear warmup, then linear decay (default)
    bf16=True,                             # FA2 + bf16 throughout
    eval_strategy="steps",
    eval_steps=0.05,                       # NanoBEIR every 5% of training
    save_strategy="steps",
    save_steps=0.05,
    save_total_limit=5,
    load_best_model_at_end=True,
    metric_for_best_model="eval_NanoBEIR_R100_mean_ndcg@10",
    seed=12,
)
```

只有学习率和全局 batch size 因模型尺寸而异。

| 尺寸 | 学习率 | 全局 batch size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17m | 2.4e-4 | 1024 |
| 32m | 1.2e-4 | 512 |
| 68m | 3e-5 | 256 |
| 150m | 1.5e-5 | 192 |
| 400m | 7e-6 | 256 |
| 1b | 3e-6 | 512 |

`global_batch_size` = `per_device_batch_size x world_size x gradient_accumulation_steps`。在单个 8 卡节点上，17m 的 1024 全局 batch 意味着 `per_device=128`；在 8 个节点上意味着 `per_device=8`。训练脚本按 `global_batch_size // world_size` 计算 `per_device_batch_size`，因此同一脚本在任何节点数下都能用。全局 batch size 本可以设得更整齐一致，但我觉得上表这些数值效果很好，不想仅为一致性而重新调参。

### 评估

训练期间我监控 NanoBEIR 平均 NDCG@10（每 5% 步评估一次），并将其作为 `load_best_model_at_end` 的 `metric_for_best_model`。NanoBEIR 很快，每次训练我能负担 20 轮评估。训练结束后，我在完整的 MTEB(eng, v2) Retrieval 基准上分别评估了最佳 checkpoint（按 NanoBEIR 选出）和最后的 checkpoint。最终发布选的是 MTEB 表现更好的那个。除 68m（最后 checkpoint 略强）外，其余尺寸发布的都是 NanoBEIR 优选的那个。

### 完整训练脚本

完整脚本（每个发布模型所用的就是它）是一个单文件。每次运行只改 `ENCODER_SIZE`，其他全自动：

```
from __future__ import annotations

import logging
import os
from pathlib import Path

import torch
import torch.nn as nn
from datasets import concatenate_datasets, get_dataset_config_names, load_dataset

from sentence_transformers import CrossEncoder
from sentence_transformers.base.modules import Dense
from sentence_transformers.cross_encoder import (
    CrossEncoderModelCardData,
    CrossEncoderTrainer,
    CrossEncoderTrainingArguments,
)
from sentence_transformers.cross_encoder.evaluation import CrossEncoderNanoBEIREvaluator
from sentence_transformers.cross_encoder.losses import MSELoss
from sentence_transformers.sentence_transformer.modules import LayerNorm, Pooling, Transformer

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO, format="%(asctime)s %(message)s", datefmt="%H:%M:%S")
logging.getLogger("httpx").setLevel(logging.WARNING)

# Per-size config. I swept the learning rates with these global (effective) batch sizes,
# also by incorporating accum_steps
CONFIGS: dict[str, dict] = {
    "17m":  {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-17m",  "learning_rate": 2.4e-4, "global_batch_size": 1024},
    "32m":  {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-32m",  "learning_rate": 1.2e-4, "global_batch_size": 512},
    "68m":  {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-68m",  "learning_rate": 3e-5,   "global_batch_size": 256},
    "150m": {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-150m", "learning_rate": 1.5e-5, "global_batch_size": 192},
    "400m": {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-400m", "learning_rate": 7e-6,   "global_batch_size": 256},
    "1b":   {"base_model_name": "jhu-clsp/ettin-encoder-1b",   "learning_rate": 3e-6,   "global_batch_size": 512},
}
ENCODER_SIZE = "17m"

def main() -> None:
    config = CONFIGS[ENCODER_SIZE]
    encoder_id = config["base_model_name"]
    learning_rate = config["learning_rate"]
    global_batch_size = config["global_batch_size"]

    world_size = int(os.environ.get("WORLD_SIZE", 1))
    per_device_batch_size = global_batch_size // world_size
    dataloader_workers = 0 if world_size > 8 else 4
    run_name = f"ettin-reranker-{ENCODER_SIZE}-lr{learning_rate:.0e}"

    # 1. Load a model to finetune with model card data
    # The model mirrors ModernBertForSequenceClassification, but with a 'headless' Transformer that just loads
    # AutoModel. This allows for unpadding with FA2, which isn't possible with AutoModelForSequenceClassification.
    # This speeds up training considerably, while heavily reducing memory usage.
    torch.manual_seed(12)
    transformer = Transformer(encoder_id, model_kwargs={"attn_implementation": "flash_attention_2"})
    transformer.model.config.num_labels = 1
    embedding_dimension = transformer.get_embedding_dimension()
    pooling = Pooling(embedding_dimension=embedding_dimension, pooling_mode="cls")
    dense_inner = Dense(
        in_features=embedding_dimension, out_features=embedding_dimension, bias=False,
        activation_function=nn.GELU(),
        module_input_name="sentence_embedding", module_output_name="sentence_embedding",
    )
    norm = LayerNorm(dimension=embedding_dimension)
    dense_score = Dense(
        in_features=embedding_dimension, out_features=1, bias=True,
        activation_function=nn.Identity(),
        module_input_name="sentence_embedding", module_output_name="scores",
    )
    model = CrossEncoder(
        modules=[transformer, pooling, dense_inner, norm, dense_score],
        num_labels=1,
        activation_fn=nn.Identity(),
        model_card_data=CrossEncoderModelCardData(
            model_name=f"Ettin Reranker {ENCODER_SIZE} distilled from mxbai-rerank-large-v2",
            language="en",
            license="apache-2.0",
        ),
    )
    actual_attn = getattr(model[0].model.config, "_attn_implementation", None)
    if not (actual_attn and "flash" in actual_attn.lower()):
        logging.warning(f"FA2 may not be active (attn_impl={actual_attn!r}); training will be slower.")

    # 2. Load the dataset. Each config is one source subset (32 lighton + 7 rerank retrieval
    # domains). The held-out eval rows live as the 'validation' split of the 'quora' config.
    dataset_repo = "cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data"
    train_pieces = []
    eval_dataset = None
    for config_name in get_dataset_config_names(dataset_repo):
        dataset = load_dataset(dataset_repo, config_name)
        train_pieces.append(dataset["train"])
        if "validation" in dataset:
            eval_dataset = dataset["validation"]
    train_dataset = concatenate_datasets(train_pieces)
    print(train_dataset)

    # 3. Define a loss function
    loss = MSELoss(model)

    # 4. Specify training arguments
    args = CrossEncoderTrainingArguments(
        output_dir=f"models/{run_name}",
        num_train_epochs=1,
        per_device_train_batch_size=per_device_batch_size,
        per_device_eval_batch_size=per_device_batch_size,
        gradient_accumulation_steps=1,
        learning_rate=learning_rate,
        warmup_ratio=0.03,
        bf16=True,
        eval_strategy="steps",
        eval_steps=0.05,
        save_strategy="steps",
        save_steps=0.05,
        save_total_limit=5,
        logging_steps=0.025,
        logging_first_step=True,
        load_best_model_at_end=True,
        metric_for_best_model="eval_NanoBEIR_R100_mean_ndcg@10",
        dataloader_num_workers=dataloader_workers,
        run_name=run_name,
        seed=12,
    )

    # 5. Create an evaluator
    evaluator = CrossEncoderNanoBEIREvaluator(
        dataset_names=["msmarco", "nfcorpus", "nq", "fiqa2018", "touche2020", "scifact",
                       "hotpotqa", "arguana", "fever", "dbpedia", "climatefever", "scidocs",
                       "quoraretrieval"],
        batch_size=per_device_batch_size,
        always_rerank_positives=False,
        show_progress_bar=False,
    )

    # 6. Create a trainer
    trainer = CrossEncoderTrainer(
        model=model,
        args=args,
        train_dataset=train_dataset,
        eval_dataset=eval_dataset,
        loss=loss,
        evaluator=evaluator,
    )

    # 7. Evaluate before training
    if trainer.is_world_process_zero():
        with torch.autocast(device_type="cuda", dtype=torch.bfloat16):
            evaluator(model)

    # 8. Train
    trainer.train()

    # 9. Evaluate the final model
    if trainer.is_world_process_zero():
        with torch.autocast(device_type="cuda", dtype=torch.bfloat16):
            evaluator(model)

    # 10. Save the final model
    final_dir = f"models/{run_name}/final"
    model.save_pretrained(final_dir)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

多节点训练（除 17m/32m 外的所有尺寸）用 `torchrun` 启动同一脚本：

```
# Single-node (17m, 32m): defaults work
python train.py

# Multi-node 4n setup for 150m, preserves global_batch_size=192:
torchrun --nproc_per_node=8 --nnodes=4 ... train.py
```

## 结论

ettin-reranker-v1 系列只用了单一而简单的配方训练，却在直至 1B 参数的每个发布尺寸上都达到最先进水平。从一个强教师做 pointwise MSE 蒸馏，应用到"宽领域 + 检索专用"的混合数据上，从 17M 到 1B 干净扩展——尺寸之间只需改学习率和每设备 batch size。

每个 ettin-reranker-v1 模型在 MTEB 和 NanoBEIR 上都以可观优势胜过 `ms-marco-MiniLM-L*-v2` 系列。`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-150m-v1` 是我在 600M 以下档位测过的最强中档重排器，`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-400m-v1` 距 1.54B 教师的 MTEB 分数只差 0.0024，`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1` 与教师只差 0.0001。

一切汇总在一处：

- **模型**：[`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-17m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-17m-v1) [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-32m-v1) [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-68m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-68m-v1) [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-150m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-150m-v1) [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-400m-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-400m-v1) [`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-1b-v1)
- **数据集**：[`cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data)，约 1.43 亿 `(query, document, label)` 三元组，保留为 39 个具名 split，每一行的来源都可追溯。
- **训练脚本**：上文[完整训练脚本](https://huggingface.co/blog/ettin-reranker#overall-training-script)的约 150 行，六个模型用的都是它。

如果你基于它们做了什么，请告诉我！我真的很想看看大家会用它们做出什么；如果你能用发布的数据训出更好的重排器，那就更好了。这套配方是刻意保持简单的，部分原因是给别人留出充足的改进空间。训一个更强的教师，同一份脚本就能持续产出更强的学生。

> If I understand well, you are mixing lightOn hard negatives data (with Jang et al. stratified sampling) with broader lightonai/embeddings-pre-training - which (i think) doesn't include mined negatives

Correct on all counts. They're different data formats initially. I took:

- query-answer pair style data from [https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-pre-training](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-pre-training) together with
- [https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-fine-tuning](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lightonai/embeddings-fine-tuning) + rescoring with mxbai-rerank-large-v2 + Jang et al. stratified sampling + subsampled to 64 docs per query + reformatted to 64 query-answer pairs.

Yes, I did try various mixes. Specifically, I tried multiple approaches for the second subsampling (256 -> 64), e.g. grabbing 16 random docs, grabbing top 8 + 8 random, top 16 + 16 random, top 16 + 16 uniformly sampled, top 32, top 64, and what worked best for me: 32 top + 32 uniformly subsampled.

I also tried different mixes of the pre-training data vs the embeddings-fine-tuning data. As you can see [https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data#lighton-pre-training-32-configs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-v1-data#lighton-pre-training-32-configs), I limited the largest sources from embeddings-pre-training to 5M and smaller ones to 2.5M, but during my tests I also tried limiting the largest to 1M and the smallest to 500k. Expanding to the larger number of rows was beneficial. I might also benefit from going larger, but that was exceeding my training time goals. I only experimented with the number of embeddings-fine-tuning data by updating its sampling (e.g. the top 64 has 2x as much fine-tuning data as the top 32 test).

I ended up not wanting to do too many experiments on the data mixture, as I was wary that I might start to overfit on my evaluation benchmark (NanoBEIR), which would only result in better NanoBEIR scores but worse overall MTEB scores. So I didn't do too much.

> In ST, you advise to discard Arguana and Touché when using Nanobeir13, I guess it works best with those at the end ?

In truth, I probably should not have excluded these 2 from the default CrossEncoderNanoBEIREvaluator, as people might run the dense and reranker evaluators side by side and expect to be able to compare them, which you now sadly can't do. But in short: At the time, my opinion was that Arguana and Touche2020 didn't really match the usual usage for rerankers. For example, in ArguAna the query is just a big paragraph, and in Touche the documents are a bit vague (i.e. not as confident information sources but instead opinions). I think these might make them less suited as benchmarking datasets for reranker models, and I suppose also less interesting as training datasets for training rerankers. In the blogpost, I included them, as I want the scores to be easier to compare to the dense NanoBEIR13 scores from a standard embedding model.
