People and AI agents need context to get work done, but it’s usually scattered across systems, from Slack to Google Docs. Finding it and understanding how it all fits together can be a challenge.

That’s why [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.co/) is building a knowledge intelligence platform that unifies those sources and turns raw context into verified knowledge available wherever people and agents work.

OpenAI models like GPT‑6 Astra and GPT‑6 Sol power the platform’s AI, with tasks routing to different models based on the reasoning required and cost. Codex helps the company’s engineers build the products, while a ChatGPT plugin makes their newer agentic product, Stack Overflow for Agents, easier to discover and use.

> “We built a ChatGPT plugin for Stack Overflow for Agents, and our registrations tripled overnight.”

—Alexandra Lato, VP of Product, Stack Overflow

## Two engineers ship a net-new product in a few weeks with Codex

Stack Overflow for Agents, which extends Stack’s intelligence layer to agents, began with a hypothesis and two engineers using Codex. The engineers gave Codex a development environment and a plan, and then worked with it to build the product. Within a few weeks, Alex says, they had a full-fledged product.

“So it really made their team feel bigger,” Alex says. “They were able to throw a container at it and say, ‘This is our plan, help us build it.’”

## Developing connectors in days, not weeks, with Codex

Stack Internal, Stack Overflow’s enterprise product, needs to connect to all the systems where organizations keep their knowledge. Building this capability requires more human involvement than the two-engineer effort behind Stack Overflow for Agents, but Codex is accelerating this work, too.

Alex describes using Codex to pursue a much shorter path to market for the enterprise product: three months rather than a year. Building connectors is one concrete example.

> “On average, it takes us 3 to 6 weeks to build one connector. With Codex, we were able to build it within 4 days.”

—Alexandra Lato, VP of Product, Stack Overflow

More recently, the team built a connector in a single day. By accelerating connectors, Codex helps Stack Overflow bring enterprise products to market faster.

## Increasing agent creation and signups with a ChatGPT plugin

Once Stack Overflow for Agents was ready for distribution, the team needed a way to get it into people’s workflows. It built a ChatGPT plugin so users could discover and set up the product in ChatGPT, with fewer steps than a traditional Model Context Protocol (MCP) setup.

> “Once you have a plugin, it becomes a frictionless setup with ChatGPT.”

—Alexandra Lato, VP of Product, Stack Overflow

Alex says the plugin tripled registrations and accelerated agent creation: “I think we were up to 10X last time I checked.”

## Reducing configuration work with the Decisions API

Stack Overflow manages its own model routing and configuration, but Alex hopes to explore using the Decisions API to reduce some of that work and put more engineering capacity into the product.

“If we’re able to automate that through OpenAI directly, that saves us a ton of time and money, allows us to focus more on the actual product we’re building rather than the guardrails around it,” Alex says.

## Expanding across the full knowledge lifecycle

Stack Overflow is also working on expanding its knowledge product to enable sharing of what people learn. To date, its focus has been pulling information in. Now, Alex is closing the loop so knowledge gained in a one-to-one session with a developer or someone outside engineering can help others, too.

“As I’m learning, how do I make sure that the rest of my organization is learning with me?”, said Alex. “I’m really looking forward to building a product that allows for that full knowledge life cycle.”
